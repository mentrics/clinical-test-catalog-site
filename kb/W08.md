# CD-RISC-10 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Resilience (Well-being)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 40

## What this test is

CD-RISC-10 (Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale-10) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **resilience**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. CD-RISC-10: sum of 10 items (the 10-item subset of the 25-item CD-RISC). Validation (Campbell-Sills & Stein 2007): alpha = .85; distinguishes trauma-exposed from non-exposed and tracks treatment change. **Conceptual note: CD-RISC measures protective TRAITS (tenacity, optimism, tolerance of negative affect) rather than recovery-per-se — see BRS (W07) for the recovery-focused alternative.** No clinical cutoffs — continuous trait measure.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
