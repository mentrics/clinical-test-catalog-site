# ULS-8 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Loneliness short form (Social)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 8 items · **Scores from 8 to 32

## What this test is

ULS-8 (UCLA Loneliness Scale — 8-item version (ULS-8)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **loneliness short form**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 8 items (1 item reverse-keyed per official ULS-8 key). Validation (Hays & DiMatteo 1987): alpha = .87+. Interpretation: higher = greater loneliness; no official clinical cutoffs.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Hays & DiMatteo 1987).
