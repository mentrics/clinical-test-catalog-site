# BRIEF-A — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Executive function (Attention/Executive)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report/informant

**Length:** 75 items · **Scores from 0 to 9

## What this test is

BRIEF-A (Behavior Rating Inventory of Executive Function — Adult) is a self-report/informant questionnaire that measures **executive function**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. BRIEF-A (75 items): 9 clinical scales (Inhibit, Shift, Emotional Control, Self-Monitor, Initiate, Working Memory, Plan/Organize, Task Monitor, Organization of Materials) → 2 index scores (Behavioral Regulation Index, Emergent Metacognition Index) + Global Executive Composite. Scale scores = raw sums converted to T-scores against gender/age norms (M = 50, SD = 10); T >=65 = clinically elevated. Validation (Roth et al. 2005): internal consistency .80-.90 across scales.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–9 | **T-score interpretation** | Scales + indices interpreted as T-scores; T >=65 = clinically elevated executive dysfunction. Official BRIEF-A norm tables (gender + age strata) required for conversion. |
