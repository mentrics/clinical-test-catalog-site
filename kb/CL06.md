# ASRM — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Mania symptoms (Clinical)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 5 items · **Scores from 0 to 20

## What this test is

ASRM (Altman Self-Rating Mania Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **mania symptoms**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. ASRM: 5 items mapping to 5 DSM mania domains (elevated mood, increased self-esteem, decreased need for sleep, pressured speech, subjective energy). Sum all 5. Validation (Altman et al. 1997): alpha = .86; cutoff 6+ discriminates mania from controls (sensitivity .93, specificity .91 vs clinician SADS-C).

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Below threshold** | Manic symptoms not clinically significant |
| 6–20 | **Manic symptoms indicated** | 6+ — possible mania/hypomania; clinical evaluation indicated |
