# SPIN — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Social anxiety (Anxiety)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 17 items · **Scores from 0 to 68

## What this test is

SPIN (Social Phobia Inventory) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **social anxiety**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Not at all (0), A little bit (1), Somewhat (2), Very much (3), Extremely (4).

<details><summary>Example items (17 total)</summary>

- I am afraid of people in authority.
- I am bothered by blushes in front of people.
- Parties and social events scare me.
- I avoid talking to people I don't know.
- Being criticized scares me a lot.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 17 items. Original validation (Connor et al. 2000): alpha = .94; cutoff >=19 distinguished social phobia from controls (sensitivity ~80%, specificity ~88%); >=26 in later ROC work for probable social anxiety disorder. Anti-SPIN (fear of negative evaluation subset) is a 10-item variant.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–18 | **Below screening threshold** | Social anxiety symptoms not prominent |
| 19–25 | **Elevated social anxiety** | At original cutoff; assess for social phobia |
| 26–68 | **Probable social anxiety disorder range** | High symptom burden; clinical assessment indicated |
