# RRS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Rumination (Repetitive Thinking)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 22 items · **Scores from 1 to 4

## What this test is

RRS (Ruminative Responses Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **rumination**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. RRS (22 items original): total mean + 3 subscales per Treynor et al. (2003) revision — Depression-related Brooding (5), Depression-related Reflection (5), and 12 symptom items (dropped in the revised 10-item RRS-10). Score = mean per subscale. Validation (Treynor et al. 2003): brooding alpha = .74, reflection alpha = .79. Brooding (passive comparison of one's situation to unachievable standards) is the maladaptive facet; reflection (purposeful contemplation) is more neutral. No clinical cutoffs.

### Subscales

- **Brooding (maladaptive)** — mean
- **Reflection (adaptive-ish)** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Nolen-Hoeksema & Morrow 1991; Treynor, Gonzalez & Nolen-Hoeksema 2003 for RRS factor revision).
