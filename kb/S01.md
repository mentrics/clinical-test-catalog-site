# PSQI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Sleep quality (Sleep)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 19 items · **Scores from 0 to 21

## What this test is

PSQI (Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **sleep quality**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Component scoring: 19 self-rated questions collapse into 7 components (subjective quality, latency, duration, efficiency, disturbances, medication, daytime dysfunction), each 0-3; global = sum of components 0-21. Original validation (Buysse et al. 1989): alpha = .83, test-retest r = .85; cutoff >5 distinguishes good vs poor sleepers (sensitivity 89.6%, specificity 86.5%).

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Good sleep quality** | Below clinical-threshold sleep disturbance |
| 6–10 | **Poor sleep quality** | Above cutoff (>5): poor sleep quality; examine component profile |
| 11–21 | **Severe sleep disturbance** | High global disturbance; clinical sleep evaluation warranted |
