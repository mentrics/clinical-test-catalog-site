# IRI-EC — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Empathic concern (Interpersonal)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 7 items · **Scores from 0 to 4

## What this test is

IRI-EC (Interpersonal Reactivity Index — Empathic Concern subscale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **empathic concern**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. IRI-EC: 7 items of the IRI's Empathic Concern subscale (affective empathy: warmth, compassion, concern for others). Score = mean after reverse-keying per official IRI key. Validation (Davis 1983): alpha = .78 for EC subscale. No clinical cutoffs — cognitive-affective empathy dimensions reported separately in the full IRI (I01).

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Davis 1980, 1983); IRI-EC = the 7-item empathic-concern subscale used standalone.
