# DEBQ — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Emotional eating (Eating)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 33 items · **Scores from 13 to 65

## What this test is

DEBQ (Dutch Eating Behavior Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **emotional eating**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. DEBQ: 33 items, 3 subscales — Emotional Eating (13), External Eating (10), Restrained Eating (10). Subscale scores = sums (or means) after reverse-keying where applicable per official key. Validation (van Strien, Frijters, Bergers & Defares 1986): alphas .80-.95 across subscales; distinct factor structure confirmed cross-culturally. No clinical cutoffs — measures eating-style traits.

### Subscales

- **Emotional Eating** — sum
- **External Eating** — sum
- **Restrained Eating** — sum

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
