# ASSIST — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Substance screening (Substance)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Interview

**Length:** 8 items · **Scores from 0 to 20

## What this test is

ASSIST (Alcohol, Smoking and Substance Involvement Screening Test) is a interview questionnaire that measures **substance screening**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. ASSIST covers 10 substance categories (tobacco, alcohol, cannabis, cocaine, amphetamine, inhalants, sedatives, hallucinogens, opioids, other). For EACH substance: Q1-7 scored, summed → substance-specific involvement score (0-20 for most, tobacco 0-15 scale interpretation differs). Cutoffs (WHO ASSIST manual v3; Humeniuk et al. 2008): tobacco 4+ moderate risk, alcohol 11+ moderate risk, other drugs 2+ moderate risk; moderate = brief intervention, high = intensive treatment.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Low risk (per substance)** | No intervention needed (per-substance scoring; tobacco threshold 3, alcohol 10, others 3 per WHO manual) |
| 4–26 | **Moderate risk (per substance)** | Brief intervention indicated (tobacco 4+, alcohol 11+, others 2+) |
| 27–100 | **High risk (per substance)** | Intensive treatment indicated (alcohol 27+; tobacco 16+; others 27+) |
