# PDSS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Panic (Anxiety)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Clinician

**Length:** 7 items · **Scores from 0 to 28

## What this test is

PDSS (Panic Disorder Severity Scale — Self-Report) is a clinician questionnaire that measures **panic**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. 7 items map to 7 DSM-5 panic-disorder dimensions: panic attacks, distress about attacks, anticipatory anxiety, agoraphobic avoidance, interoceptive avoidance, panic-related sensory symptoms, impairment. Subscale scores = each item (except item 5, which is item 4+5 averaged in some protocols — verify). Total = sum (or sum of 7 dimensions when items are aggregated). Validation (Houck et al. 2002): alpha = .80; excellent agreement with clinician PDSS.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | **Not clinically significant** | Below panic-disorder threshold |
| 5–11 | **Mild to moderate panic disorder** | At/above common clinical threshold (5+) |
| 12–17 | **Moderate to severe panic disorder** | Moderate-severe panic |
| 18–28 | **Severe panic disorder** | Severe panic; active treatment indicated |
