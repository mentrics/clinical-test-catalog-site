# ASRS v1.1 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Adult ADHD (Attention/Executive)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 18 items · **Scores from 0 to 24

## What this test is

ASRS v1.1 (Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS v1.1) Symptom Checklist) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **adult adhd**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Never (0), Rarely (1), Sometimes (2), Often (3), Very Often (4).

<details><summary>Example items (18 total)</summary>

- How often do you have trouble wrapping up the final details of a project, once the challenging parts have been done?
- How often do you have difficulty getting things in order when you have to do a task that requires organization?
- How often do you have problems remembering appointments or obligations?
- When you have a task that requires a lot of thought, how often do you avoid or delay getting started?
- How often do you fidget or squirm with your hands or feet when you have to sit down for a long time?

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. TWO uses. (1) Screener: the 6 dark-shaded items (Part A: items 1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 13 of the official form) are scored 0/1 by their own anchors (Never/Rarely=0; Sometimes=1; Often/Very Often=1 per anchor mapping in official form) — 4+ shaded items positive = screen positive. (2) Symptom scale: sum all 18 items 0-4 for severity (0-72 range in research use). This file records the 18-item checklist; the official screener uses the shaded subset.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Screen negative** | Fewer than 4 shaded items positive — ADHD screening criteria not met |
| 4–6 | **Screen positive** | 4+ shaded items positive — full ADHD evaluation indicated (screen is not diagnostic) |
