# GSE — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Self-efficacy (Well-being)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 10 to 40

## What this test is

GSE (General Self-Efficacy Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **self-efficacy**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 10 items (no reverse-keyed items). Validation (Schwarzer & Jerusalem 1995; Scholz et al. 2002 25-nation study): alphas .76-.90 across cultures. Interprets as a continuous general self-efficacy measure; higher = stronger belief in one's coping capacity. No clinical cutoffs.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Schwarzer & Jerusalem 1995).
