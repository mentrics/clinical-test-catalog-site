# Brief COPE — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Coping strategies (Coping)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 28 items · **Scores from 1 to 4

## What this test is

Brief COPE (Brief COPE) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **coping strategies**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. Brief COPE: 28 items in 14 subscales of 2 items each (Self-distraction, Active coping, Denial, Substance use, Use of emotional support, Use of instrumental support, Behavioral disengagement, Venting, Positive reframing, Planning, Humor, Acceptance, Religion, Self-blame). Score = mean per 2-item subscale (some items reverse-keyed per official key). No total score standard — subscale profile is the output. Validation (Carver 1997): alphas .50-.90 (self-distraction lowest, religion highest); many 2-item scales have modest reliability.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
