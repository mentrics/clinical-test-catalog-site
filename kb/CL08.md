# BSL-23 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Borderline symptom severity (Clinical)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 23 items · **Scores from 0 to 92

## What this test is

BSL-23 (Borderline Symptom List-23) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **borderline symptom severity**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. BSL-23 (short form of BSL-95): sum of all 23 items. Higher = greater BPD-symptom severity. Validation (Bohus et al. 2009): alpha = .97; discriminates BPD patients from other psychiatric patients with high accuracy. Sensitivity-to-change established for dialectical-behavior-therapy outcome research. Cutoffs (Bohus et al. 2009): >=23 suggests clinically relevant BPD symptoms; norm tables in the BSL manual refine this.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–22 | **Below clinically-relevant threshold** | BPD symptoms not prominent |
| 23–92 | **Clinically relevant BPD symptoms** | At/above 23 — BPD-symptom severity; clinical assessment indicated |
