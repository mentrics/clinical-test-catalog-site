# ECR-RS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Adult attachment (Relationships)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 9 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

ECR-RS (Experiences in Close Relationships Scale — Relationships Structures) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **adult attachment**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. ECR-RS: 9 items, 2 subscales — Anxiety (6 items) and Avoidance (3 items)... wait, ECR-RS standard: 9 items per relationship target (4 anxiety + 5 avoidance or similar per official key — VERIFY). Designed for MULTIPLE relationship targets (mother, father, romantic partner, best friend) — the respondent rates each target separately, producing per-target scores. Validation (Fraley et al. 2011): alphas .82-.90 (partner), .81-.92 (other targets). No clinical cutoffs — dimensional.

### Subscales

- **Anxiety (per target)** — mean
- **Avoidance (per target)** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
