# OBQ-44 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Obsessive beliefs (Anxiety)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 44 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

OBQ-44 (Obsessive Beliefs Questionnaire-44) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **obsessive beliefs**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. OBQ-44: 44 items, 3 factor scales — Responsibility/Threat Estimation (16 items), Perfectionism/Certainty (14), Importance/Control of Thoughts (14). Score = mean per factor scale (no total standard — the 3 factors are the output). Validation (OCCWG 2003, 2005): alphas .89-.95 across factor scales. No clinical cutoffs — measures OCD-relevant belief domains (cognitive model of OCD).

### Subscales

- **Responsibility / Threat Estimation** — mean
- **Perfectionism / Certainty** — mean
- **Importance / Control of Thoughts** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
