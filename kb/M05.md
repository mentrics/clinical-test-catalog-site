# MADRS-S — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Depression (Mood)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 9 items · **Scores from 0 to 27

## What this test is

MADRS-S (Montgomery-Åsberg Depression Rating Scale — Self-report) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **depression**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 9 items (self-report version uses 9 of the 10 clinician items, dropping 'observed sadness'). Original MADRS validation (Montgomery & Åsberg 1979): sensitivity to treatment change established. MADRS-S validation (Svanborg & Åsberg 1994): alpha = .88, correlation with clinician MADRS r = .87.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | **Normal** | No depressive syndrome |
| 7–19 | **Mild depression** | Mild depression |
| 20–34 | **Moderate depression** | Moderate depression |
| 35–60 | **Severe depression** | Severe depression (clinician-version bands; MADRS-S bands analogous at 0-27 range) |
