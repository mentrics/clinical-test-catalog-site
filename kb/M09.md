# SHAPS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Anhedonia (Mood)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 14 items · **Scores from 0 to 12

## What this test is

SHAPS (Snaith-Hamilton Pleasure Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **anhedonia**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 4 items (each statement keyed so agreement = anhedonia). Validation (Snaith et al. 1995): alpha = .87-.90 in clinical and control samples; cutoff >=2 in original paper suggests anhedonia (sensitivity/specificity balanced in psychiatric samples). SHAPS is the most widely used brief anhedonia measure.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | **No anhedonia** | Below screening threshold |
| 2–12 | **Anhedonia indicated** | At/above original cutoff — assess for depression and loss of pleasure capacity |
