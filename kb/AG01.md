# STAS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Trait/state anger (Anger)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 44 items · **Scores from 8 to 32

## What this test is

STAS (State-Trait Anger Expression Inventory-2) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **trait/state anger**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. STAXI-2 (57 items): State Anger (2 subscales, 10 items), Trait Anger (2 subscales, 10 items), Anger Expression-Out (8), Anger Expression-In (8), Anger Control-Out (6), Anger Control-In (6), Anger Expression Index (16, composite). Scale scores = sums interpreted as T-scores against gender-specific norms. Validation (Spielberger 1999): alphas .73-.93 across scales. T >=70 = clinically elevated.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 8–32 | **T-score interpretation** | Scales interpreted as T-scores (M = 50, SD = 10); T >=70 = clinically elevated. Official STAXI-2 norm tables required for conversion. |
