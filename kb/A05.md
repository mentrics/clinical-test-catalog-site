# LSAS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Social anxiety (Anxiety)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Clinician/self-report

**Length:** 24 items · **Scores from 0 to 144

## What this test is

LSAS (Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale) is a clinician/self-report questionnaire that measures **social anxiety**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum Fear (0-72) and Avoidance (0-72) separately, plus total (0-144). Validation (Liebowitz 1987; Fresco et al. 2001 psychometric refinement): alpha total = .96; cutoffs for severity bands widely applied from Rytwinski et al. (2009) ROC analysis. LSAS 30+ is a common clinical/screening threshold; 55+ / 65+ / 80+ for moderate/moderate-severe/severe bands.

### Subscales

- **Fear/Anxiety** — sum
- **Avoidance** — sum

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | **Below threshold** | Social anxiety not prominent |
| 30–54 | **Moderate social anxiety** | At common clinical threshold (30+) |
| 55–64 | **Marked social anxiety** | Marked social phobia range |
| 65–79 | **Severe social anxiety** | Severe social phobia range |
| 80–144 | **Very severe social anxiety** | Very severe range |
