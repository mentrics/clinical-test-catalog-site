# AHS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Hope (Positive Psychology)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 12 items · **Scores from 1 to 8

## What this test is

AHS (Adult Hope Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **hope**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. AHS: 12 items — Agency (4, goal-directed energy), Pathways (4, perceived capacity to find routes), 4 fillers (not scored). Score = mean per subscale (agency, pathways) + total hope (mean of 8 scored items). Validation (Snyder et al. 1991): alphas Agency .71-.79, Pathways .80-.84. No clinical cutoffs.

### Subscales

- **Agency** — mean
- **Pathways** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Snyder et al. 1991).
