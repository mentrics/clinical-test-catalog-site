# CGI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Global severity (Clinical)

**Who it's for:** Clinical adults · **How it's given:** Clinician

**Length:** 3 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

CGI (Clinical Global Impression (Severity + Improvement)) is a clinician questionnaire that measures **global severity**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. CGI-S and CGI-I are SEPARATE single-item clinician ratings — not summed. CGI-S rates current severity 1-7; CGI-I rates change since baseline 1-7 (lower = improved). Interpretation is categorical/ordinal — no T-scores or norms. CGI-I of 1 (very much improved) or 2 (much improved) is the conventional treatment-response criterion in clinical trials. Requires a trained clinician familiar with the disorder context.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1–7 | **Ordinal clinician rating** | CGI-S: 1 normal, 2 borderline ill, 3 mildly ill, 4 moderately ill, 5 markedly ill, 6 severely ill, 7 most extremely ill. CGI-I: 1-2 = response, 3 = minimal improvement, 4 = no change, 5-7 = worse. |
