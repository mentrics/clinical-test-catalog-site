# IGDS9-SF — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Internet gaming disorder (Behavioral Addiction)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 9 items · **Scores from 9 to 45

## What this test is

IGDS9-SF (Internet Gaming Disorder Scale 9-Short Form) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **internet gaming disorder**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. IGDS9-SF: 9 items mapping to DSM-5 IGD criteria (preoccupation, tolerance, withdrawal, persistence, displacement, deception, escape, conflict, displacement-conflict). Total = sum (higher = greater IGD severity). Validation (Pontes & Griffiths 2015): alpha = .89, one-factor structure confirmed; correlates with general problematized gaming. No official diagnostic cutoff — the instrument is dimensional (IGD itself is a DSM-5 condition-for-further-study, not an established diagnosis).

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
