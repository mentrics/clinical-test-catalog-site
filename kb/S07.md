# AIS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Insomnia severity (Sleep)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 8 items · **Scores from 0 to 24

## What this test is

AIS (Athens Insomnia Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **insomnia severity**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 8 items (5 sleep-continuity + 2 daytime symptoms + 1 duration). Validation (Soldatos et al. 2000): cutoff 6+ for insomnia diagnosis per DSM-IV (sensitivity 93%, specificity 85%); cutoff 10 for maximizing specificity in some applications. 5-item version exists (AIS-5) for screening.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Below threshold** | Insomnia criteria not met |
| 6–9 | **Positive screen (original cutoff)** | At/above 6 — insomnia indicated (93%/85% sens/spec) |
| 10–24 | **Severe insomnia** | High symptom burden; active treatment indicated |
