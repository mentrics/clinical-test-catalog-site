# ECR-R — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Adult attachment (Relationships)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 36 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

ECR-R (Experiences in Close Relationships Scale-Revised) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **adult attachment**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. ECR-R: 36 items, 2 subscales of 18 items each — Attachment Anxiety (anxious/preoccupied) and Attachment Avoidance (dismissive/avoidant). Subscale scores = means after reverse-keying ~half the items per scale per official key. Validation (Fraley et al. 2000): alphas .90-.95 across subscales. ECR-R is dimensional (not categorical) — the 4 attachment 'styles' are derived via quadrant classification (high/low anxiety x high/low avoidance) in some research conventions, not inherent scoring. No clinical cutoffs.

### Subscales

- **Attachment Anxiety** — mean
- **Attachment Avoidance** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
