# CTS2 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Conflict tactics (Relationships)

**Who it's for:** Couples · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 78 items · **Scores from 0 to 39

## What this test is

CTS2 (Revised Conflict Tactics Scales) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **conflict tactics**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. CTS2 (39 items): 5 scales — Negotiation (6 items), Psychological Aggression (8), Physical Assault (12), Sexual Coercion (7), Injury (6). Per-scale scoring can be prevalence (any nonzero response) or frequency (sum, capped at 25 per item to limit skew). **The CTS2 is a violence/abuse measure — scoring output is per-scale prevalence/frequency, not a single total. Interpretation requires clinical context.** Validation (Straus et al. 1996): internal consistency .79-.89 across scales.

### Subscales

- **Negotiation** — sum or prevalence
- **Psychological Aggression** — sum or prevalence
- **Physical Assault** — sum or prevalence
- **Sexual Coercion** — sum or prevalence
- **Injury** — sum or prevalence

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–39 | **Per-scale prevalence/frequency** | No total score — 5 scales scored independently (prevalence = any occurrence; frequency = sum, capped at 25/item). Any nonzero physical assault, sexual coercion, or injury response warrants clinical safety assessment. |
