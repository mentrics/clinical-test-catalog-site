# PIL — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Purpose in life (Well-being)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 20 items · **Scores from 14 to 98

## What this test is

PIL (Purpose in Life Test — Short Form) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **purpose in life**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. PIL-SF: 14 items (short form of the 20-item PIL), sum 14-98. Validation (Schulenberg 2004 for PIL-SF): internal consistency ~.80-.90; correlates with MLQ Presence and well-being. No clinical cutoffs — higher = greater sense of purpose/meaning.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
