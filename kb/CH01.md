# CBCL — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Broad psychopathology (Child/Adolescent)

**Who it's for:** Children/adolescents · **How it's given:** Parent report

**Length:** 113 items · **Scores from 0 to 240

## What this test is

CBCL (Child Behavior Checklist (6-18)) is a parent report questionnaire that measures **broad psychopathology**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. CBCL/6-18 (112 scored items): 8 syndrome scales (Anxious/Depressed, Withdrawn/Depressed, Somatic Complaints, Social Problems, Thought Problems, Attention Problems, Rule-Breaking Behavior, Aggressive Behavior) → Internalizing + Externalizing broadband factors + Total Problems score. Scale scores = raw sums converted to T-scores against gender/age normative samples (M = 50, SD = 10); T >=65 = borderline, T >=70 = clinically significant. Validation (Achenbach & Rescorla 2001): alphas .63-.97 across scales.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–240 | **T-score interpretation** | Scales + broadband factors interpreted as T-scores: T 60-63 borderline, T 64-69 borderline clinical, T 70+ clinically significant (conventional ASEBA thresholds). Official ASEBA norm tables (gender/age/rater) required for conversion. |
