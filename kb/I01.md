# IRI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Empathy (Interpersonal)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 28 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

IRI (Interpersonal Reactivity Index) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **empathy**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. IRI: 28 items, 4 subscales of 7 items each — Perspective Taking (PT), Fantasy (FS), Empathic Concern (EC), Personal Distress (PD). Subscale scores = means after reverse-keying (several items per subscale reverse-keyed per official key). Validation (Davis 1983): alphas .71-.82 across subscales; 4-factor structure confirmed. No clinical cutoffs — cognitive (PT, FS) vs affective (EC, PD) empathy are reported separately.

### Subscales

- **Perspective Taking (cognitive)** — mean
- **Fantasy (cognitive)** — mean
- **Empathic Concern (affective)** — mean
- **Personal Distress (affective)** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
