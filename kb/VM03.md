# BREQ-3 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Behavioral regulation (Values/Motivation)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 24 items · **Scores from 0 to 76

## What this test is

BREQ-3 (Behavioral Regulation in Exercise Questionnaire-3) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **behavioral regulation**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. BREQ-3: 19 items in 5 subscales — Amotivation (4), External regulation (4), Introjected regulation (4), Identified regulation (4), Integrated regulation (3). Subscale scores = sums (or means); higher subscale = more of that regulation style. Validation (Markland & Tobin 2004 BREQ-2; Wilson et al. 2006 for integrated regulation): alphas .74-.89 across subscales. Self-Determination-Continuum interpretation: higher amotivation/external = less self-determined; identified/integrated = more self-determined exercise motivation.

### Subscales

- **Amotivation** — sum
- **External regulation** — sum
- **Introjected regulation** — sum
- **Identified regulation** — sum
- **Integrated regulation** — sum

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Markland & Tobin 2004 for BREQ-2; Wilson et al. 2006 for integrated regulation addition in BREQ-3).
