# FCQ-T — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Food craving (Eating)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 39 items · **Scores from 39 to 234

## What this test is

FCQ-T (Food Cravings Questionnaire — Trait) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **food craving**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. FCQ-T (39 items): 9 subscales — Cues Triggered by Food Thoughts (4 items), Hunger (4), Anticipated Positive Reinforcement (5), Anticipated Relief from Negative States (4), Lack of Control over Eating (6), Preoccupation with Food (4), Emotions/Triggering of Cravings (4), Guilt from Cravings (4), Physiological Cravings (4). Score = mean or sum per subscale (no total standard). Validation (Cepeda-Benito, Gleaves, Williams & Erath 2000): alphas .79-.93 across subscales.

### Subscales

- **Cues Triggered by Food Thoughts** — mean
- **Hunger** — mean
- **Anticipated Positive Reinforcement** — mean
- **Anticipated Relief from Negative States** — mean
- **Lack of Control over Eating** — mean
- **Preoccupation with Food** — mean
- **Emotions/Triggering of Cravings** — mean
- **Guilt from Cravings** — mean
- **Physiological Cravings** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
