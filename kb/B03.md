# MBI-GS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Burnout (Stress/Burnout)

**Who it's for:** Adults/workers · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 16 items · **Scores from 0 to 6

## What this test is

MBI-GS (Maslach Burnout Inventory — General Survey) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **burnout**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. MBI-GS: 16 items, 3 subscales — Exhaustion (5), Cynicism (5), Professional Efficacy (6, reverse-keyed for total interpretation). Subscale scores = means (0-6 each). Validation (Maslach, Jackson & Leiter 1996; Schaufeli et al. 1996 for GS): alphas .80-.87 across subscales. Burnout pattern = high Exhaustion + high Cynicism + low Efficacy (no single cutoff — the MBI is interpreted dimensionally; some countries adopt exhaustion mean >=3.0 as 'at risk').

### Subscales

- **Exhaustion** — mean
- **Cynicism** — mean
- **Professional Efficacy** — mean

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | **Dimensional interpretation** | No official total cutoff — burnout is a PATTERN (high exhaustion + high cynicism + low efficacy). Some national guidelines use Exhaustion >=3.0 mean as a risk indicator; check local policy. |
