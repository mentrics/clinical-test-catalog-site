# GQ-6 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Gratitude (Positive Psychology)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 6 items · **Scores from 6 to 42

## What this test is

GQ-6 (Gratitude Questionnaire-6) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **gratitude**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 6 items (2 reverse-keyed: items 3 and 6 — the agree/disagree items about gratitude not applying). Validation (McCullough et al. 2002): alpha = .82, test-retest r = .67. Higher = greater dispositional gratitude. No clinical cutoffs.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (McCullough, Emmons & Tsang 2002).
