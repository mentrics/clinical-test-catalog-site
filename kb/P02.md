# CAPE-42 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Psychosis experiences (Psychosis/Bipolar)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 42 items · **Scores from 0 to 168

## What this test is

CAPE-42 (Community Assessment of Psychic Experiences — 42 items) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **psychosis experiences**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. CAPE-42 has 3 positive-symptom-dimension subscales (Positive symptoms 20 items, Negative symptoms 14 items, Depressive symptoms 8 items), each scored for FREQUENCY and DISTRESS separately (both 42-item grids), giving 6 subscale scores. Total is a research convention; subscale + distress scores are primary. Validation (Stefanis et al. 2002): alphas positive .89, negative .85, depressive .86 (frequency scale). Cutoffs: percentile-based from general-population norms (Konings et al. 2006); no clinical diagnostic cutoff.

### Subscales

- **Positive symptoms — frequency** — sum
- **Positive symptoms — distress** — sum
- **Negative symptoms — frequency** — sum
- **Negative symptoms — distress** — sum
- **Depressive symptoms — frequency** — sum
- **Depressive symptoms — distress** — sum

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Free for research/clinical use with attribution (Stefanis et al. 2002).
