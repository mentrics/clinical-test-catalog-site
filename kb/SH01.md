# C-SSRS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Suicide risk (Suicide/Self-harm)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Clinician/interview

**Length:** Variable items · **Scores from 0 to 6

## What this test is

C-SSRS (Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale) is a clinician/interview questionnaire that measures **suicide risk**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. C-SSRS Screener: 6 yes/no items (wish to be dead, active ideation, method, intent, plan, behavior). The screener is RISK-STRATIFIED by item pattern rather than summed: items 1-2 = low risk (monitor); item 3 (method) = moderate; item 4 (intent) = high; items 5-6 (plan, behavior) = very high risk. Full C-SSRS rates ideation severity (1-5) and behavior (actual/aborted/interrupted attempts) clinically.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | **Low risk** | Ideation without intent/plan — monitor, consider referral |
| 2–3 | **Moderate risk** | Method considered — safety assessment indicated |
| 4–5 | **High risk** | Intent or plan — immediate safety evaluation indicated |
| 6–6 | **Very high risk** | Attempt behavior (actual/aborted/interrupted) — emergency evaluation |

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Free for clinical/research use (Columbia Lighthouse Project); no fee, registration requested. Screener version is the most common clinical use.
