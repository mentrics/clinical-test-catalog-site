# MoCA — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Global cognition (Cognition)

**Who it's for:** Older adults/adults · **How it's given:** Performance

**Length:** 30 items · **Scores from 0 to 30

## What this test is

MoCA (Montreal Cognitive Assessment) is a performance questionnaire that measures **global cognition**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. MoCA: sum of 8 domain scores (max 30); +1 point if education <=12 years. Validation (Nasreddine et al. 2005): alpha = .83; MoCA superior to MMSE for mild cognitive impairment detection. Cutoffs (Nasreddine et al. 2005; updated normative data): <26 = cognitive impairment (original threshold; sensitivity .90 for MCI, specificity .87); 26-30 = normal; new multi-domain normative interpretation is increasingly preferred over the fixed 26 threshold.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–25 | **Cognitive impairment indicated** | <26 — cognitive impairment likely; further assessment indicated |
| 26–30 | **Normal range** | 26+ — within normal range (education-adjusted) |
