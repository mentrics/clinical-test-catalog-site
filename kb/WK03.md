# MBI-HSS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Burnout (Work)

**Who it's for:** Healthcare/service workers · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 22 items · **Scores from 0 to 6

## What this test is

MBI-HSS (Maslach Burnout Inventory — Human Services Survey) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **burnout**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. MBI-HSS (22 items): 3 subscales — Emotional Exhaustion (9), Depersonalization (5), Personal Accomplishment (8, reverse-interpreted). Subscale scores = means (0-6 each). Validation (Maslach & Jackson 1981): alphas .83-.90 across subscales. Burnout pattern = high EE + high DP + low PA. Original MBI-HSS high-risk bands (Maslach & Jackson 1981, norms for human-service workers): EE >=27 high, DP >=10 high, PA <=33 high (low accomplishment); NO total score — the 3-subscale pattern is the interpretation frame.

### Subscales

- **Emotional Exhaustion** — mean
- **Depersonalization** — mean
- **Personal Accomplishment** — mean

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | **Dimensional 3-subscale interpretation** | No total score. High-risk pattern (Maslach & Jackson 1981 human-services norms): Emotional Exhaustion >=27, Depersonalization >=10, Personal Accomplishment <=33. Moderate bands: EE 17-26, DP 6-9, PA 34-39. |
