# CSI-16 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Relationship quality (Relationships)

**Who it's for:** Couples · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 16 items · **Scores from 0 to 96

## What this test is

CSI-16 (Couple Satisfaction Index-16) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **relationship quality**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. CSI-16: sum of 16 items (higher = greater satisfaction). Validation (Funk & Rogge 2007): alpha = .98; CSI-16 provides ~50% more precision than the DAS-32 in distinguishing distressed from non-distressed couples. Cutoffs (Funk & Rogge 2007): <=51 = distressed couple (sensitivity .85, specificity .93 vs DAS threshold). CSI-4 and CSI-32 variants exist.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–51 | **Distressed couple** | <=51 — clinically significant relationship distress (Funk & Rogge 2007) |
| 52–96 | **Non-distressed** | Above distress threshold |
