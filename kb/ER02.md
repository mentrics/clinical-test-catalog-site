# ERQ-S — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Emotion regulation short form (Emotion Regulation)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 6 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

ERQ-S (Emotion Regulation Questionnaire — Short Form (ERQ-S)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **emotion regulation short form**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. ERQ-S (Preece, Becerra, Hasking et al. 2020): 6 items, 2 subscales — Reappraisal (3 items) and Suppression (3 items). Score = mean per subscale. Validation (Preece et al. 2020): alphas .75-.79 (comparable to full ERQ). **Audit finding (consolidate.md): Rilev uses 'ERQ-S' — the AGENTS.md version-ambiguity list flags this. ### Subscales

- **Reappraisal** — mean
- **Suppression** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution; ERQ-S is a 6-item short form of the 10-item ERQ (Preece et al. 2020).
