# ESS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Daytime sleepiness (Sleep)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 8 items · **Scores from 0 to 24

## What this test is

ESS (Epworth Sleepiness Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **daytime sleepiness**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 8 items. Validation (Johns 1991): alpha = .88, test-retest r = .82. Cutoffs (Johns 1991; updated in later work): 0-5 lower normal daytime sleepiness, 6-10 higher normal, 11-12 mild excessive, 13-15 moderate, 16-24 severe excessive sleepiness. ESS is a daytime-sleepiness measure (not insomnia) — complements ISI/PSQI.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | **Normal daytime sleepiness** | 0-5 lower normal, 6-10 higher normal |
| 11–12 | **Mild excessive sleepiness** | Mild excessive daytime sleepiness; monitor |
| 13–15 | **Moderate excessive sleepiness** | Moderate EDS; sleep evaluation indicated |
| 16–24 | **Severe excessive sleepiness** | Severe EDS; sleep-medicine referral strongly indicated |
