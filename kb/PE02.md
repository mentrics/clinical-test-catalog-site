# BFI-2 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Big Five (Personality)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 60 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

BFI-2 (Big Five Inventory-2) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **big five**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. Domain scores = mean of items, AFTER reverse-scoring (60 items: 5 domains x 12 items; ~40% reverse-keyed). Three 4-item facets per domain (15 facets total). Validation (Soto & John 2017): domain alphas .82-.90, 3-month test-retest ~.86. No clinical cutoffs — normative interpretation by sample percentiles.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Free for research and clinical use with attribution (Soto & John).
