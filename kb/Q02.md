# Sheehan Disability Scale — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Global functioning (Functioning)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 3 items · **Scores from 0 to 30

## What this test is

Sheehan Disability Scale (Sheehan Disability Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **global functioning**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. 3 subscales of 1 item each (Work/school, Social life, Family life/home responsibilities) + optional 4th (days lost). Score = sum of the 3 (0-30) or mean (0-10). Validation (Leon, Olfson, Portera, Farber & Sheehan 1997): test-retest r = .80s; discriminates treatment-seeking from non-treatment-seeking samples. No formal diagnostic cutoffs; higher = greater disability.

### Subscales

- **Work/school impairment** — single item 0-10
- **Social life impairment** — single item 0-10
- **Family life/home impairment** — single item 0-10

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for clinical/research use (Leon et al. 1997).
