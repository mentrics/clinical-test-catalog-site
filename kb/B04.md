# Oldenburg Burnout Inventory — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Burnout (Stress/Burnout)

**Who it's for:** Adults/workers · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 16 items · **Scores from 1 to 4

## What this test is

Oldenburg Burnout Inventory (Oldenburg Burnout Inventory) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **burnout**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. OLBI: 16 items, 2 subscales of 8 items each — Exhaustion (E) and Disengagement (D). Subscale scores = means after reverse-keying (about half the items per subscale are phrased positively). Validation (Demerouti & Bakker 2008): alphas .74-.86 across samples. OLBI's exhaustion scale captures both physical and cognitive fatigue (wider than MBI's emotional focus). No official clinical cutoffs — dimensional; higher = greater burnout.

### Subscales

- **Exhaustion** — mean
- **Disengagement** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Demerouti 1998/2003).
