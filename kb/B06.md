# DASS-42 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Depression/anxiety/stress (Stress/Burnout)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 42 items · **Scores from 0 to 126

## What this test is

DASS-42 (Depression Anxiety Stress Scales-42) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **depression/anxiety/stress**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. DASS-42: 42 items, 3 subscales of 14 items each (Depression, Anxiety, Stress). Subscale sums 0-42 each; total 0-126. Validation (Lovibond & Lovibond 1995): alphas Depression .94, Anxiety .87, Stress .91. Cutoffs (Lovibond & Lovibond 1995 manual, Table 5): Depression normal 0-9, mild 10-13, moderate 14-20, severe 21-27, extremely severe 28+; Anxiety normal 0-7, mild 8-9, moderate 10-14, severe 15-19, extremely severe 20+; Stress normal 0-14, mild 15-18, moderate 19-25, severe 26-33, extremely severe 34+.

### Subscales

- **Depression** — sum
- **Anxiety** — sum
- **Stress** — sum

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–126 | **Per-subscale bands** | DASS-42 reports 3 subscale scores (0-42 each, no doubling needed — the 21-item version requires x2 to align), each banded separately: Depression 0-9/10-13/14-20/21-27/28+; Anxiety 0-7/8-9/10-14/15-19/20+; Stress 0-14/15-18/19-25/26-33/34+. See Lovibond & Lovibond (1995) manual Table 5. |
