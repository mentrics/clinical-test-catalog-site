# UCLA Loneliness Scale v3 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Loneliness (Social)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 20 items · **Scores from 20 to 80

## What this test is

UCLA Loneliness Scale v3 (UCLA Loneliness Scale — Version 3) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **loneliness**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 20 items (9 reverse-keyed per official key). Validation (Russell 1996): alpha = .89-.94, test-retest r = .73 (12 months). Interpretation: continuous measure; no official clinical cutoffs, but research commonly treats 60+ as high loneliness. Version-ambiguity note (AGENTS.md): catalog carries v3 — Russell 1996 explicitly distinguishes v3 from v1 (1978) and v2 (1980).

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Russell 1996); widely reproduced.
