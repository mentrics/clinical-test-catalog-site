# SCS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Self-compassion (Well-being)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 26 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

SCS (Self-Compassion Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **self-compassion**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. 26 items in 6 subscales (Self-Kindness, Self-Judgment reverse, Common Humanity, Isolation reverse, Mindfulness, Over-identification reverse). Total = mean of all 26 items AFTER reverse-scoring the 3 negative subscales. Validation (Neff 2003): alpha = .92. Short form SCS-SF (12 items, Raes et al. 2011) alpha = .86+. No clinical cutoffs — continuous measure.

### Subscales

- **Self-Kindness** — mean
- **Self-Judgment** — mean
- **Common Humanity** — mean
- **Isolation** — mean
- **Mindfulness** — mean
- **Over-identification** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Neff 2003); self-compassion.org hosts the scale.
