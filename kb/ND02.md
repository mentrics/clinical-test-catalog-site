# AQ-10 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Autistic traits short form (Neurodevelopment)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 10

## What this test is

AQ-10 (Autism Spectrum Quotient-10) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **autistic traits short form**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. 10 items (4 attention-switching, 4 social skill, 2 attention to detail) keyed per official sheet. Validation (Allison et al. 2012): cutoff >=6 in adult clinical triage (sensitivity .88, specificity .91 for DSM-IV ASD in high-functioning adults). NICE guidance endorses AQ-10 as first-stage triage in UK adult autism pathways.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Below triage threshold** | Autism triage criteria not met |
| 6–10 | **Positive triage** | At/above 6 — full autism diagnostic assessment indicated (NICE pathway) |

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Free for clinical/research use with attribution (Allison, Auyeung & Baron-Cohen); NHS/NICE-endorsed triage in UK adult services.
