# SPS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Social anxiety (Anxiety)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 20 items · **Scores from 0 to 80

## What this test is

SPS (Social Phobia Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **social anxiety**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 20 items. Companion to SIAS — measures fear of being observed (scrutiny) rather than interaction anxiety. Validation (Mattick & Clarke 1998): alpha = .89-.94; SPS and SIAS correlate ~.6-.7 but load on separate factors.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–23 | **Below threshold (informal)** | Scrutiny fear not prominent (heuristic) |
| 24–80 | **Elevated scrutiny fear (informal)** | At/above commonly applied research threshold |
