# BSQ-34 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Body image (Eating)

**Who it's for:** Adults/adolescents · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 34 items · **Scores from 16 to 96

## What this test is

BSQ-34 (Body Shape Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **body image**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. BSQ-34: sum of all 34 items. Validation (Cooper, Taylor, Cooper & Fairburn 1987): alpha = .97; test-retest r = .88. Cutoffs (Cooper et al. 1987; Evans & Dolan 1993 refinement): <81 no body-image concern, 81-110 mild, 111-140 moderate, 141+ severe body-image concern.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 16–80 | **No body-image concern** | Below mild threshold |
| 81–110 | **Mild body-image concern** | Mild distress about body shape |
| 111–140 | **Moderate body-image concern** | Moderate distress; clinical attention indicated |
| 141–96 | **Severe body-image concern** | Severe distress; clinical intervention indicated |
