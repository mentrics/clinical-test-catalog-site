# DAST-10 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Drug use (Substance)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 10

## What this test is

DAST-10 (Drug Abuse Screening Test-10) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **drug use**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum 'yes' answers. Validation (Skinner 1982 original DAST-20; Yudko, Hall & Hirshbein 2007 for DAST-10): alpha = .95 (DAST-20); DAST-10 correlates .97 with DAST-20. Cutoffs (Yudko et al. 2007): 1-2 low, 3-5 moderate, 6-8 substantial, 9-10 severe.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–0 | **No drug use problem** | No items endorsed |
| 1–2 | **Low level** | Low-level drug-use concern |
| 3–5 | **Moderate level** | Moderate drug-use concern; brief intervention indicated |
| 6–8 | **Substantial level** | Substantial problem; intensive assessment indicated |
| 9–10 | **Severe level** | Severe problem; intensive treatment indicated |
