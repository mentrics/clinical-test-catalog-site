# Flourishing Scale — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Flourishing (Well-being)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 8 items · **Scores from 7 to 49

## What this test is

Flourishing Scale (Flourishing Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **flourishing**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 8 items. Validation (Diener et al. 2010): alpha = .87-.89; correlates strongly with SWLS, PERMA, and meaning measures. Interpretation bands are informal (no official cutoffs — the scale is norm-referenced in research). Suggested heuristic bands: <30 relatively low flourishing; 30-43 moderate; 44+ high.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 7–29 | **Low flourishing (informal)** | Below typical adult range; consider context |
| 30–43 | **Moderate flourishing (informal)** | Typical range |
| 44–49 | **High flourishing (informal)** | High self-reported flourishing |
