# CAPS-5 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** PTSD clinician rating (Trauma)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Clinician interview

**Length:** 30 items · **Scores from 0 to 80

## What this test is

CAPS-5 (Clinician-Administered PTSD Scale for DSM-5) is a clinician interview questionnaire that measures **ptsd clinician rating**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. The CAPS-5 is the gold-standard CLINICAL INTERVIEW for PTSD. Severity = sum of the 20 symptom items (0-80). Diagnosis requires BOTH (1) symptom items scored >=2 (moderate) per cluster rules (B: >=1, C: >=1, D: >=2, E: >=2) AND (2) onset/duration/impairment items positive. The 30-33 range is a widely used severity-indication threshold, but the CAPS-5's primary output is the categorical diagnosis. Validation (Weathers et al. 2018): excellent inter-rater reliability (ICC .89-.97), alpha .88.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | **Below threshold** | Below typical severity-indication range (but diagnosis depends on rules, not total alone) |
| 30–80 | **Indicated severity range** | At/above 30-33 conventional severity indication; diagnostic determination per cluster + impairment rules |

## Usage & licensing

**Public domain** — US government work (VA National Center for PTSD); free to use. Requires trained clinician administration — not a self-report instrument.
