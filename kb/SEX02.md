# GMSEX — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Sexual satisfaction (Sexuality)

**Who it's for:** Adults/couples · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 5 items · **Scores from 5 to 35

## What this test is

GMSEX (Global Measure of Sexual Satisfaction) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **sexual satisfaction**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. GMSEX: 5 items (bipolar adjectives: very good/very bad, very pleasant/very unpleasant, very positive/very negative, very satisfied/very unsatisfied, very worthless/very worthwhile — anchor pairs per official form). Sum 5-35 (higher = greater satisfaction). Validation (Lawrence & Byers 1995): alpha = .93; correlates strongly with relationship satisfaction. No clinical cutoffs.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
