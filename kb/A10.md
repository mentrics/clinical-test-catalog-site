# IUS-12 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Intolerance of uncertainty (Anxiety)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 12 items · **Scores from 12 to 60

## What this test is

IUS-12 (Intolerance of Uncertainty Scale-12) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **intolerance of uncertainty**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Two-factor structure: Inhibitory Anxiety (7 items) and Prospective Anxiety (5 items). Sum each + total. Validation (Carleton, Norton & Asmundson 2007): alphas .85 (inhibitory), .80 (prospective), .90 (total). Cutoffs vary by study; total 35+ commonly treated as elevated intolerance of uncertainty in clinical research.

### Subscales

- **Inhibitory anxiety** — sum
- **Prospective anxiety** — sum

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 12–34 | **Below elevated threshold (informal)** | IU not prominent (heuristic; no universal official cutoff) |
| 35–60 | **Elevated intolerance of uncertainty (informal)** | At/above commonly applied research threshold |
