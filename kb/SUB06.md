# FTND — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Nicotine dependence (Substance)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 6 items · **Scores from 0 to 10

## What this test is

FTND (Fagerström Test for Nicotine Dependence) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **nicotine dependence**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. FTND: 6 items (3 scored 0-1, 3 scored 0-2 or 0-3 — item anchors vary; e.g., smoking within 30 min of waking = 1, first cigarette timing; cigarettes per day 0-3). Sum all 6. Validation (Heatherton et al. 1991): alpha = .61 (low for 6 items, expected for heterogeneous content); discriminates dependence severity in clinical trials. Cutoffs (Heatherton et al. 1991): 0-2 low dependence, 3-4 low-to-moderate, 5+ moderate-to-high dependence.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | **Low dependence** | Low nicotine-dependence indicators |
| 3–4 | **Low-to-moderate dependence** | Low-to-moderate dependence |
| 5–10 | **Moderate-to-high dependence** | Moderate-to-high dependence; cessation support indicated |
