# IIP-32 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Interpersonal problems (Interpersonal)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 32 items · **Scores from 0 to 4

## What this test is

IIP-32 (Inventory of Interpersonal Problems-32) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **interpersonal problems**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. IIP-32: 32 items in 8 subscales of 4 items each (Dominant, Vindictive, Cold, Socially Avoidant, Nonassertive, Exploitable, Overly Nurturant, Intrusive), arranged around the interpersonal circumplex (agency x communion axes). Subscale scores = means; circumplex octant profile is the interpretive frame. Validation (Horowitz et al. 1988; Alden, Wiggins & Pincus 1990 circumplex structure): alphas .75-.89 across subscales. No clinical cutoffs — interpret relative to normative samples.

### Subscales

- **Dominant** — mean
- **Vindictive** — mean
- **Cold** — mean
- **Socially Avoidant** — mean
- **Nonassertive** — mean
- **Exploitable** — mean
- **Overly Nurturant** — mean
- **Intrusive** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
