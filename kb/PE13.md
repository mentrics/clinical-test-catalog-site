# MACH-IV — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Machiavellianism (Personality)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 20 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

MACH-IV (Mach-IV Machiavellianism Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **machiavellianism**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. Mach-IV: 20 statements (10 pro-Machiavellian + 10 anti-Machiavellian); half reverse-keyed. Score = mean after keying (some versions sum). Validation (Christie & Geis 1970): test-retest r = .89; split-half .80. No clinical cutoffs — Machiavellianism as a trait measure (higher = more manipulative/cynical view of interpersonal relations).

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use (Christie & Geis 1970, Machiavellianism and other social-psychological perspectives).
