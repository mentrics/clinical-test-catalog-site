# MHC-SF — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Positive mental health (Positive Psychology)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 14 items · **Scores from 0 to 70

## What this test is

MHC-SF (Mental Health Continuum — Short Form) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **positive mental health**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. MHC-SF: 14 items in 3 subscales — Emotional well-being (3), Psychological well-being (6), Social well-being (5). Score = sum per subscale + total (higher = more flourishing). Validation (Keyes 2005; Lamers et al. 2011): alphas .80-.87 across subscales. Categorical classification (Keyes 2005): flourishing = 1 of 2 emotional-wellbeing items AND 6+ of 14 total items at 4-5; moderate = not flourishing + not languishing; languishing = 0-1 emotional item at 4-5 AND 1-5 total items at 4-5.

### Subscales

- **Emotional well-being** — sum
- **Psychological well-being** — sum
- **Social well-being** — sum

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–70 | **Categorical classification (Keyes 2005)** | Flourishing / moderate / languishing per Keyes (2005) categorical algorithm — 1+ of 2 emotional items at 4-5 AND 6+ of 14 total at 4-5 = flourishing; 0-1 emotional item at 4-5 AND 1-5 total at 4-5 = languishing; otherwise moderate. |
