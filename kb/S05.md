# MEQ — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Chronotype (Sleep)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 19 items · **Scores from 0 to 70

## What this test is

MEQ (Morningness-Eveningness Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **chronotype**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. MEQ: sum of 19 items (item anchors assign 0-6 points each per Horne & Östberg key). Validation (Horne & Östberg 1976): alpha = .84; correlates with body-temperature rhythm phase. Cutoffs (Horne & Östberg 1976): 16-30 definite evening, 31-41 moderate evening, 42-58 neither, 59-69 moderate morning, 70-86 definite morning. (Max is 86 with the original key — catalog range 0-70 may be a variant; VERIFY.)

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 | **Definite/moderate evening type** | Evening chronotype |
| 31–41 | **Moderate evening type** | Moderately evening |
| 42–58 | **Neither (intermediate)** | Intermediate chronotype |
| 59–70 | **Morning type** | Morning chronotype |
