# MSPSS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Perceived social support (Social)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 12 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

MSPSS (Multidimensional Scale of Perceived Social Support) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **perceived social support**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. 3 subscales of 4 items each (Family, Friends, Significant Other) + total. Score = mean per subscale and overall (some conventions sum 12-84 — report mean 1-7 or sum; document which). Validation (Zimet et al. 1988): alphas .85-.91 across subscales; overall .88. Cutoffs (Canty-Mitchell & Zimet 2000): mean <=3.0 low support, 3.0-5.0 moderate, >=5.0 high.

### Subscales

- **Family** — mean
- **Friends** — mean
- **Significant Other** — mean

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **Low social support** | Mean <=3.0 — low perceived support (Canty-Mitchell & Zimet 2000 bands) |
| 3.01–5 | **Moderate social support** | Mean 3-5 — moderate perceived support |
| 5.01–7 | **High social support** | Mean >=5.0 — high perceived support |
