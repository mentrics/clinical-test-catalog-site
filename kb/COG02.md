# MMSE — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Global cognition (Cognition)

**Who it's for:** Adults/older adults · **How it's given:** Performance

**Length:** 30 items · **Scores from 0 to 30

## What this test is

MMSE (Mini-Mental State Examination) is a performance questionnaire that measures **global cognition**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. MMSE: sum of 11 domains (max 30). Validation (Folstein, Folstein & McHugh 1975): widely used dementia screen. Cutoffs (Folstein 1975; adjusted for education/MMSE normative work, Crum et al. 1993): <24 = cognitive impairment indicated; 24-30 = normal range; severe impairment <10; education-adjusted norms preferred. MMSE is less sensitive to MCI than MoCA.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–9 | **Severe cognitive impairment** | <10 — severe impairment |
| 10–23 | **Cognitive impairment indicated** | 10-23 — moderate impairment range; assessment indicated |
| 24–30 | **Normal range** | 24+ — within conventional normal range (education-adjusted norms preferred) |
