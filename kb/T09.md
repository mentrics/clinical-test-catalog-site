# PTGI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Posttraumatic growth (Trauma)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 21 items · **Scores from 0 to 105

## What this test is

PTGI (Posttraumatic Growth Inventory) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **posttraumatic growth**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 21 items, plus 5 subscales: Relating to Others (7), New Possibilities (5), Personal Strength (4), Spiritual Change (2), Appreciation of Life (3). Validation (Tedeschi & Calhoun 1996): alphas .67-.85 across subscales. PTGI measures POSITIVE post-trauma change (growth), not distress — higher = more reported growth. No diagnostic cutoffs; interpretation is relative to comparison samples.

### Subscales

- **Relating to Others** — sum
- **New Possibilities** — sum
- **Personal Strength** — sum
- **Spiritual Change** — sum
- **Appreciation of Life** — sum

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
