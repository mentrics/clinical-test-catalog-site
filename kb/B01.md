# PSS-10 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Perceived stress (Stress/Burnout)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 40

## What this test is

PSS-10 (Perceived Stress Scale-10) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **perceived stress**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 10 items (4 reverse-keyed: items 4, 5, 7, 8 — 'positive' statements about control/coping). Validation (Lee 2012 systematic review): alpha = .74-.91 across versions. No official clinical cutoffs — interpret relative to normative samples (US adult mean ≈ 13-14 in Cohen's samples; higher = greater perceived stress).

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
