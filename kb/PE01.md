# IPIP-NEO-120 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Big Five (Personality)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 120 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

IPIP-NEO-120 (International Personality Item Pool — NEO 120-item version) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **big five**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. IPIP-NEO-120: 5 domains x 24 items + 30 facets x 4 items each (facet items sum to domain). Domain + facet scores = means after reverse-keying per official IPIP key (ipip.ori.org). Approximately half the items are reverse-keyed. Psychometrics: Johnson (2014) reports comparable validity to NEO-PI-R; internal consistency facets .70s, domains .90+.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — IPIP items are in the public domain (ipip.ori.org — 'no permission needed'); free for any use with attribution to the IPIP project.
