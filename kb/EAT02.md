# EDE-QS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Eating disorder short form (Eating)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 12 items · **Scores from 0 to 72

## What this test is

EDE-QS (Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire Short) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **eating disorder short form**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: No days / Not at all (0), 1-5 days / Slightly (1), 6-12 days / Mildly (2), 13-15 days / Moderately (3), 16-22 days / Markedly (4), 23-27 days / Markedly+ (5), Every day / Markedly++ (6).

<details><summary>Example items (12 total)</summary>

- Have you been deliberately trying to limit the amount of food you eat to influence your shape or weight (whether or not you have succeeded)?
- Have you gone for long periods of time (8 waking hours or more) without eating anything at all in order to influence your shape or weight?
- Have you tried to exclude from your diet any foods that you like in order to influence your shape or weight?
- Have you had a definite sense that you have lost control over eating (at the time that you were eating)?
- On how many of the past 7 days...have you had a sense of losing control over your eating (at the time that you were eating)?

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 12 items. Validation (Gideon, Hawkes & Waller 2013 via PLOS ONE): alpha = .913, ICC = .93; strong correlation with full EDE-Q. Cutoff >=16 suggested by validation work (sensitivity/specificity balance for probable eating-disorder case); interpret alongside clinical interview.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | **Below threshold** | Eating-disorder features not prominent |
| 16–72 | **At/above threshold** | Probable eating-disorder features; full EDE-Q/clinical assessment indicated |
