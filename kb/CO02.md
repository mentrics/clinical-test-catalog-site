# COPE — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Ways of coping (Coping)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 60 items · **Scores from 1 to 4

## What this test is

COPE (COPE (full)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **ways of coping**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. COPE (full, 60 items): 15 subscales of 4 items each (Active coping, Planning, Suppression of competing activities, Restraint coping, Seeking social support — instrumental, Seeking social support — emotional, Positive reinterpretation, Acceptance, Denial, Turning to religion, Behavioral disengagement, Venting, Substance use, Humor, Mental disengagement). Score = mean per subscale. Validation (Carver et al. 1989): alphas .62-.92 across subscales (restraint lowest). No total score standard — subscale profile is the output.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
