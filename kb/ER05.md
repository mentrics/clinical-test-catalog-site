# TAS-20 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Alexithymia (Emotion Regulation)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 20 items · **Scores from 20 to 100

## What this test is

TAS-20 (Toronto Alexithymia Scale-20) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **alexithymia**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. TAS-20: 20 items, 3 subscales — Difficulty Identifying Feelings (7), Difficulty Describing Feelings (5), Externally Oriented Thinking (8). Sum each + total (about half the items reverse-keyed per official key). Validation (Bagby et al. 1994): alphas .66-.82 across subscales, .81 total. Cutoffs (Bagby et al. 1994): total >=61 = alexithymia, 53-60 = possible alexithymia, <=52 = non-alexithymia.

### Subscales

- **Difficulty identifying feelings** — sum
- **Difficulty describing feelings** — sum
- **Externally oriented thinking** — sum

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 20–52 | **Non-alexithymia** | Below screening threshold |
| 53–60 | **Possible alexithymia** | Borderline range |
| 61–100 | **Alexithymia** | At/above 61 — clinically significant alexithymia |
