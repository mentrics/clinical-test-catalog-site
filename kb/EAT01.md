# EDE-Q — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Eating disorder symptoms (Eating)

**Who it's for:** Adolescents/adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 28 items · **Scores from 0 to 120

## What this test is

EDE-Q (Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **eating disorder symptoms**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. EDE-Q: 22 scored items (of 28 total — 6 behavioral-frequency items are not summed directly) in 4 subscales of 5-6 items each (Restraint, Eating Concern, Shape Concern, Weight Concern). Subscale scores = mean of that subscale's items (0-6 each); total = mean of the 4 subscale means (0-6). Validation (Fairburn & Beglin 1994; Mond et al. 2004 psychometric update): alphas .78-.93 across subscales. Clinical threshold: EDE-Q total >=2.3-4.0 varies by study; subscale >=4 commonly flagged in screening.

### Subscales

- **Restraint** — mean
- **Eating Concern** — mean
- **Shape Concern** — mean
- **Weight Concern** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
