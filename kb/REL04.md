# DAS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Dyadic adjustment (Relationships)

**Who it's for:** Couples · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 32 items · **Scores from 0 to 151

## What this test is

DAS (Dyadic Adjustment Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **dyadic adjustment**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. DAS-32: sum of all 32 items (0-151) + 4 subscales: Dyadic Consensus (13 items, 0-65), Dyadic Satisfaction (10, 0-51), Dyadic Cohesion (5, 0-21), Affectional Expression (4, 0-12). Validation (Spanier 1976): alpha = .96 total. Cutoffs: total <=100 = dyadic distress (Spanier 1976; also Crane et al. 2000 refined to 107.5 for married couples). Higher = better adjustment.

### Subscales

- **Dyadic Consensus** — sum
- **Dyadic Satisfaction** — sum
- **Dyadic Cohesion** — sum
- **Affectional Expression** — sum

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–99 | **Dyadic distress** | Total <=100 — clinically significant relationship distress (Spanier 1976; Crane 2000 refined threshold 107.5) |
| 100–151 | **Non-distressed** | Above distress threshold |
