# DARS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Anhedonia (Mood)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 17 items · **Scores from 0 to 72

## What this test is

DARS (Dimensional Apathy Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **anhedonia**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. 24 items in 3 paired-statement subscales of 8 items each: Executive (planning/initiation), Emotional (emotional responsiveness), Initiation (behavioral activation). Subscale sums + total. Validation (Radakovic & Abrahams 2014): alphas executive .78, emotional .78, initiation .72. No diagnostic cutoffs — designed for dimensional assessment in neurodegenerative and psychiatric populations.

### Subscales

- **Executive apathy** — sum
- **Emotional apathy** — sum
- **Initiation apathy** — sum

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Free for research/clinical use with attribution (Radakovic & Abrahams 2014).
