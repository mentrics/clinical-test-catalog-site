# QIDS-SR16 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Depression severity (Mood)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 16 items · **Scores from 0 to 27

## What this test is

QIDS-SR16 (Quick Inventory of Depressive Symptomatology Self-Report 16) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **depression severity**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. 16 items → 9 DSM symptom domains (sad mood, concentration, self-criticism, anhedonia, psychomotor, appetite/weight change is 2 items scoring as one, sleep onset + middle + early morning sleep as one, energy, and pleasure/suicidal ideation per manual). Each domain takes the MAX of its component items (0-3), then the 9 domain scores are summed. Validation (Rush et al. 2003): alpha = .85, sensitivity to change established; cutoffs per Rush et al. 2003 Table 5.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **None** | No depressive syndrome |
| 6–10 | **Mild** | Mild depression |
| 11–15 | **Moderate** | Moderate depression |
| 16–20 | **Severe** | Severe depression |
| 21–27 | **Very severe** | Very severe depression |
