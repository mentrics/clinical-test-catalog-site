# WI-6 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Health anxiety (Physical Health)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 6 items · **Scores from 0 to 1

## What this test is

WI-6 (Work Instability Scale-6 (short)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **health anxiety**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. WI-6: 6 items classify work instability into 3 stages: Stage 1 (no instability), Stage 2 (at-risk), Stage 3 (unfit for work) per Lerner et al. (2001) cutoff algorithm — not a summed severity score. Validation (Lerner et al. 2001): discriminates work-disability stages in rheumatoid and general populations; used in occupational-health triage.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | **Stage classification (1/2/3)** | Stage 1 (no instability), Stage 2 (at-risk, needs accommodation), Stage 3 (unfit for current work). Classification per Lerner et al. (2001) cutoff algorithm — verify against the official WIS scoring sheet. |
