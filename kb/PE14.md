# SRP-4 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Psychopathy (Personality)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 64 items · **Scores from 1 to 4

## What this test is

SRP-4 (Self-Report Psychopathy Scale-4 — Short Form) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **psychopathy**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. SRP-4 Short Form (29 items): 4 facets (Interpersonal Affective [Manipulation, Callous Affect] and Lifestyle-Antisocial [Erratic Lifestyle, Criminal Tendencies]) scored as means after reverse-keying per official key. Total = mean of facets. Validation (Paulhus, Neumann & Hare 2016 SRP-4 manual): alphas .85-.92 across facets. Continuous trait measure — NOT a psychopathy diagnosis (diagnosis requires PCL-R clinical interview).

### Subscales

- **Manipulation** — mean
- **Callous Affect** — mean
- **Erratic Lifestyle** — mean
- **Criminal Tendencies** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
