# FSFI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Female sexual function (Sexuality)

**Who it's for:** Adult women · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 19 items · **Scores from 2 to 36

## What this test is

FSFI (Female Sexual Function Index) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **female sexual function**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. FSFI: 19 items in 6 domains — Desire (2 items, 1-5 each), Arousal (4, 0-5), Lubrication (4, 0-5), Orgasm (3, 0-5), Satisfaction (3, 0-5), Pain (3, 0-5). Domain scores = raw sum x domain-specific multiplier (Desire x0.6, Arousal/Lubrication/Orgasm/Pain x0.3, Satisfaction x0.6), each giving 0-6 (or 0-6 for Desire with 1.2 multiplier — VERIFY against official scoring sheet). Total 2-36. Validation (Wiegel et al. 2005): alpha = .82-.97 across domains. Cutoff (Wiegel et al. 2005): total <=26.55 suggests female sexual dysfunction (sensitivity .86, specificity .90).

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 2–26.55 | **Probable female sexual dysfunction** | Total <=26.55 — FSFI cutoff per Wiegel et al. (2005) ROC analysis |
| 26.56–36 | **No dysfunction indicated** | Above FSFI cutoff |
