# BFI-10 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Big Five (Personality)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

BFI-10 (Big Five Inventory-10) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **big five**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. 5 domain scores (2 items each): mean of the 2 items after reverse-keying ONE item per scale. BFI-10 halves the BFI-44 at some psychometric cost. Validation (Rammstedt & John 2007): domain alphas .63-.81 (lower than full BFI — expected for 2-item scales); acceptable for large-sample/first-pass trait estimation, not for individual diagnosis.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
