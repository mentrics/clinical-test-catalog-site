# PCL-5 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** PTSD (Trauma)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 20 items · **Scores from 0 to 80

## What this test is

PCL-5 (PTSD Checklist for DSM-5) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **ptsd**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Not at all (0), A little bit (1), Moderately (2), Quite a bit (3), Extremely (4).

<details><summary>Example items (20 total)</summary>

- Repeated, disturbing, and unwanted memories of the stressful experience?
- Repeated, disturbing dreams of the stressful experience?
- Suddenly acting or feeling as if the stressful experience were actually happening again (as if you were actually back there experiencing it again)?
- Feeling very upset when something reminded you of the stressful experience?
- Having strong physical reactions when something reminded you of the stressful experience (for example, heart pounding, trouble breathing, sweating)?

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 20 items for total severity. Cluster scores computed per DSM-5 symptom clusters. Diagnostic cutoff: 31-33 is the recommended provisional threshold; research suggests 31-33 optimal sensitivity/specificity balance. DSM-5 diagnostic rule requires >=1 item from Cluster B, >=1 from C, >=2 from D, >=2 from E scored >=2 (moderately or higher).

### Symptom clusters (DSM-5)

- **Cluster B — Intrusion** — items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5: >=1 item scored >=2
- **Cluster C — Avoidance** — items 6, 7: >=1 item scored >=2
- **Cluster D — Negative alterations in cognition/mood** — items 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14: >=2 items scored >=2
- **Cluster E — Alterations in arousal/reactivity** — items 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20: >=2 items scored >=2

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 | **Below provisional PTSD threshold** | Subthreshold; monitor, consider context |
| 31–80 | **At/above provisional PTSD threshold** | Provisional PTSD range (cutoff 31-33; VA recommends 33 for clinical settings, 31 for research screening) |
