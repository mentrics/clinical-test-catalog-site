# PHQ-9 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Depression severity (Mood)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 9 items · **Scores from 0 to 27

## What this test is

PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **depression severity**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Not at all (0), Several days (1), More than half the days (2), Nearly every day (3).

<details><summary>Example items (9 total)</summary>

- Little interest or pleasure in doing things
- Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless
- Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much
- Feeling tired or having little energy
- Poor appetite or overeating

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 9 items. PRIME-MD convention: scores >=10 suggest major depressive disorder sensitivity 88% / specificity 88% (Kroenke 2001). Item 9 (>0) as suicide-risk flag should prompt further assessment.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | **Minimal** | No significant depressive symptoms |
| 5–9 | **Mild** | Mild depression; watchful waiting, repeat at follow-up |
| 10–14 | **Moderate** | Moderate depression; consider treatment (psychotherapy, pharmacotherapy, or both) |
| 15–19 | **Moderately severe** | Moderately severe depression; active treatment warranted |
| 20–27 | **Severe** | Severe depression; pharmacotherapy and psychotherapy indicated |
