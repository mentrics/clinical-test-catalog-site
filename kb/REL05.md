# RAS — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Relationship satisfaction (Relationships)

**Who it's for:** Adults/couples · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 7 items · **Scores from 5 to 25

## What this test is

RAS (Relationship Assessment Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **relationship satisfaction**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. RAS: 7 items (item 2 reverse-keyed), sum 5-35 (5-30 if item 2 is 0-4 — confirm against the specific form; catalog lists 5 items which may be a mislabel, the RAS is 7 items). Validation (Hendrick 1988): alpha = .86. Interpretation: higher = more relationship satisfaction; no universal clinical cutoff (some research uses <=20 for distressed relationships).

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
