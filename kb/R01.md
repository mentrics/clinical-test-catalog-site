# PTQ — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Perseverative negative thinking (Repetitive Thinking)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 15 items · **Scores from 0 to 4

## What this test is

PTQ (Perseverative Thinking Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **perseverative negative thinking**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. PTQ: 15 items, 2 subscales — Repetitive Thought Content (9 items) and Mental Demands/Unproductiveness (6 items). Score = mean per subscale and total (mean of all 15). Validation (Ehring et al. 2011): alphas Repetitive Content .92, Demands .84, total .95. No clinical cutoffs — PTQ measures generic repetitive negative thinking across rumination/worry content.

### Subscales

- **Repetitive thought content** — mean
- **Mental demands / unproductiveness** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing
