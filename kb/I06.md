# Compassionate Love Scale — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Compassion (Interpersonal)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 21 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

Compassionate Love Scale (Compassionate Love Scale (for a specific close other)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **compassion**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. CLS: 21 items, single factor (compassionate love for a target person — partner/family/humanity versions). Score = mean. Validation (Sprecher & Fehr 2005): alpha = .95; discriminates from empathic concern and closeness. No clinical cutoffs.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
