# SD3 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Dark traits (Personality)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 27 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

SD3 (Short Dark Triad) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **dark traits**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. 3 subscales of 9 items each: Machiavellianism, Narcissism, Psychopathy. Subscale scores = means after reverse-keying (per-item key required). Validation (Jones & Paulhus 2014): alphas .84 (Mach), .81 (Narc), .80 (Psych). No clinical cutoffs — continuous trait measures; SD3 is a trait-measure NOT a personality-disorder diagnostic.

### Subscales

- **Machiavellianism** — mean
- **Narcissism** — mean
- **Psychopathy** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
