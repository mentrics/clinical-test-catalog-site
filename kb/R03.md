# RRS-10 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Rumination short form (Repetitive Thinking)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 1 to 4

## What this test is

RRS-10 (Ruminative Responses Scale-10) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **rumination short form**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. RRS-10 (Treynor et al. 2003 revision): 10 items in 2 subscales of 5 each — Brooding (maladaptive: passive comparison, moody rehashing) and Reflection (purposeful contemplation). Score = mean per subscale. Validation (Treynor et al. 2003): brooding alpha = .74, reflection alpha = .79; brooding (not reflection) prospectively predicts depression. No clinical cutoffs.

### Subscales

- **Brooding (maladaptive)** — mean
- **Reflection (adaptive-ish)** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Treynor, Gonzalez & Nolen-Hoeksema 2003).
