# ASRS-5 — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Adult ADHD (Attention/Executive)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 6 items · **Scores from 0 to 1

## What this test is

ASRS-5 (Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale — ASRS-5 Screener) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **adult adhd**. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. ASRS-5 (also 'ASRS 6-item screener' in some versions — confirm exact form): items are scored 0/1 by anchor mapping (Never/Rarely=0; Sometimes=1; Often/Very Often=1 for most items). POSITIVE SCREEN requires >=4 items positive (in the 6-item version) or >=3 (in some 5-item variants). The catalog lists ASRS-5 — the official ASRS-5 uses 5 items with a >=4 threshold in some versions; VERIFY the exact form against the official WHO/Harvard ASRS-5 document before scoring (threshold differs between published variants).

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–0 | **Screen negative** | Screener criteria not met |
| 1–1 | **Screen positive** | Threshold met (verify exact threshold for the specific 5-item vs 6-item form) — full ADHD evaluation indicated |
