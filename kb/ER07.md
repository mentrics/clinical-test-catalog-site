# FFMQ — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Mindfulness (Emotion Regulation)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 39 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

## What this test is

FFMQ (Five Facet Mindfulness Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **mindfulness**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. FFMQ: 39 items, 5 facets — Observing, Describing, Acting with Awareness, Non-judging of inner experience, Non-reactivity to inner experience. Facet scores = means after reverse-keying (many items across facets are reverse-keyed per official key). Validation (Baer et al. 2006): facet alphas .72-.92. FFMQ-15 short form (Baer et al. 2012) also available. No clinical cutoffs.

### Subscales

- **Observing** — mean
- **Describing** — mean
- **Acting with Awareness** — mean
- **Non-judging of inner experience** — mean
- **Non-reactivity to inner experience** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — Freely available for research use with attribution (Baer et al. 2006).
