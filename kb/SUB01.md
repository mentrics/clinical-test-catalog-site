# AUDIT — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Alcohol use (Substance)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 40

## What this test is

AUDIT (Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **alcohol use**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. Sum all 10 items. Validation (Saunders et al. 1993): alpha = .87-.94 across cultures. WHO cutoffs (Babor et al. 2001 manual): 1-7 low risk, 8-14 hazardous/harmful, 15+ dependence-likely; >=8 is the standard screening threshold (sensitivity .90, specificity .80 for hazardous use).

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 | **Low risk** | Below screening threshold |
| 8–14 | **Hazardous/harmful use** | At/above 8 — brief intervention indicated |
| 15–19 | **Risk of dependence** | 15-19 — assessment for dependence |
| 20–40 | **Dependence-likely** | 20+ — dependence assessment and intensive treatment indicated |

## Usage & licensing

**Free** — WHO instrument — free for clinical/research use with attribution (Saunders et al. 1993).
