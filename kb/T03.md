# ITQ — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** PTSD (Trauma)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 18 items · **Scores from 0 to 2

## What this test is

ITQ (International Trauma Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **ptsd**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: 0-4 per item (Not at all / A little / Moderately / Quite a bit / Extremely), past month. Items 1-6: how much the problem troubled the respondent; items 7-18: functional impairment.

<details><summary>Example items (18 total)</summary>

- Having scary thoughts or memories that come into your mind when you don't want them
- Having very upsetting nightmares
- Trying not to think about the events or have any feelings caused by the events
- Avoiding things that remind you of the events
- Being constantly 'on guard' or watchful

## How it's scored

**Method:** algorithm. The ITQ is a diagnostic instrument: 18 items map to ICD-11 PTSD (6 symptom items: 2 re-experiencing, 2 avoidance, 2 sense of current threat + functional impairment) and Complex PTSD (12 symptom items: the 6 PTSD + 6 DSO items (affective dysregulation, negative self-concept, disturbances in relationships) + impairment). An item qualifies (C) when scored 2+ (Moderately or higher). CPTSD = PTSD criteria + DSO criteria + impairment in both. Severity scores are the simple sum of clusters (research use). Validation (Cloitre et al. 2018): internal consistency .74-.89, factor structure confirmed.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–0 | **No ICD-11 PTSD/CPTSD diagnosis indicated** | Diagnostic rules not met |
| 1–1 | **PTSD (ICD-11)** | Meets ICD-11 PTSD criteria: core symptom clusters endorsed + impairment |
| 2–2 | **Complex PTSD (ICD-11)** | Meets ICD-11 CPTSD criteria: PTSD clusters + DSO clusters + impairment in both domains |
