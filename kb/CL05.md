# BSI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Brief symptom burden (Clinical)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 53 items · **Scores from 0 to 4

## What this test is

BSI (Brief Symptom Inventory) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **brief symptom burden**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. BSI (53 items, abbreviated SCL-90-R): 9 dimensions (same as SCL-90-R: Somatization, Obsessive-Compulsive, Interpersonal Sensitivity, Depression, Anxiety, Hostility, Phobic Anxiety, Paranoid Ideation, Psychoticism) + 3 global indices (GSI, PSI, PSDI). Scores = means per dimension + GSI. Interpreted as T-scores against gender-specific norms (T >=63 = clinically elevated). Validation (Derogatis & Melisaratos 1983): alphas .71-.85 across dimensions.

## What the scores mean

| Score range | Band | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | **T-score interpretation** | Dimensions + GSI interpreted as T-scores; T >=63 = clinically elevated. Use official BSI norm tables for conversion. |
