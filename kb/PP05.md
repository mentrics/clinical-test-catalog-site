# AAQ-II — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Psychological flexibility (Positive Psychology)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 7 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

## What this test is

AAQ-II (Acceptance and Action Questionnaire-II) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **psychological flexibility**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. AAQ-II: 10 items, single factor (psychological flexibility/inflexibility). Score = mean after reverse-keying (about half the items per official key). Validation (Bond et al. 2011): alpha = .88; correlates with depression, anxiety, stress across clinical and nonclinical samples. Higher (after reversal) = greater psychological flexibility (ACT measure). No clinical cutoffs.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
