# SHI — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Sleep hygiene (Sleep)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 13 items · **Scores from 13 to 65

## What this test is

SHI (Sleep Hygiene Index) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **sleep hygiene**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** sum. SHI: sum of 13 items (higher = worse sleep hygiene). Validation (Mastin et al. 2006): alpha = .53-.79 across samples (modest; item heterogeneity). Items map to 12 sleep-hygiene dimensions (caffeine, alcohol, bedtime routine, etc.) but the standard scoring is a single total. No clinical cutoffs — higher = worse hygiene practices.

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
