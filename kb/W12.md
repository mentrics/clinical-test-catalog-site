# PWB — plain-language guide

**What it measures:** Life meaning/purpose (Well-being)

**Who it's for:** Adults · **How it's given:** Self-report

**Length:** 42 items · **Scores from 1 to 6

## What this test is

PWB (Psychological Well-Being Scales (short form)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures **life meaning/purpose**. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

## Item text

The exact question wording is **not reproduced here** (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

## How it's scored

**Method:** mean. Ryff's PWB short form: 18 items in 6 subscales of 3 items each (Autonomy, Environmental Mastery, Personal Growth, Positive Relations, Purpose in Life, Self-Acceptance). Subscale scores = means after reverse-keying per official key. Validation (Ryff & Keyes 1995; Ryff 1989 original 120-item): alpha ~.70s for 3-item subscales (lower than the 20-item full forms — short form trades reliability for brevity). No clinical cutoffs — normative interpretation.

### Subscales

- **Autonomy** — mean
- **Environmental Mastery** — mean
- **Personal Growth** — mean
- **Positive Relations** — mean
- **Purpose in Life** — mean
- **Self-Acceptance** — mean

## What the scores mean

There is **no official cutoff** — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
