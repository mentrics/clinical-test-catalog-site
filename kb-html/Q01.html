WHODAS 2.0 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Disability (Functioning)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report/interview

Length: 36 items · **Scores from 0 to 144

What this test is

WHODAS 2.0 (World Health Organization Disability Assessment Schedule 2.0 (36-item)) is a self-report/interview questionnaire that measures disability. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. WHODAS 2.0 (36-item version): 6 domains — Cognition (6 items), Mobility (5), Self-care (4), Getting along (5), Life activities (8), Participation (6). Domain scores = sum (0-20 or per domain max); total = sum or IRT-scored (0-100 with transform). Validation (Ustün et al. 2010): alphas .84-.97 across domains; IRT-calibrated. Higher = greater disability. WHO publishes an IRT-based simple-score conversion (0-100) — preferred over raw sums.

Subscales

  • Cognition — sum
  • Mobility — sum
  • Self-care — sum
  • Getting along — sum
  • Life activities — sum
  • Participation — sum

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for clinical/research use (WHO, Ustün et al. 2010); available from WHO.
