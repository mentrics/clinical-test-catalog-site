BPAQ — plain-language guide

What it measures: Aggression (Interpersonal)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 29 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

What this test is

BPAQ (Buss-Perry Aggression Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures aggression. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: mean. BPAQ: 29 items, 4 subscales — Physical Aggression (9), Verbal Aggression (5), Anger (7), Hostility (8). Subscale scores = means after reverse-keying (about half the items per official key). Validation (Buss & Perry 1992): alphas Physical .85, Verbal .72, Anger .83, Hostility .77. No clinical cutoffs — trait aggression measure (continuous).

Subscales

  • Physical Aggression — mean
  • Verbal Aggression — mean
  • Anger — mean
  • Hostility — mean

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Permission — Available for research use with attribution (Buss & Perry 1992); confirm with authors for clinical use.
