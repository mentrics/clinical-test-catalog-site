MAST — plain-language guide

What it measures: Alcohol problems (Substance)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 24 items · **Scores from 0 to 24

What this test is

MAST (Michigan Alcoholism Screening Test) is a self-report questionnaire that measures alcohol problems. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. MAST: sum of 24 items (2 items weighted 2 points per some versions — verify against the specific MAST variant). Validation (Selzer 1975): test-retest r = .84. Cutoffs (Selzer 1975): 0-4 non-alcoholic, 5-6 early-to-middle problem drinker, 7+ alcoholic; 5+ is the common screening threshold.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–4 Non-alcoholic (per Selzer 1975) Below screening threshold
5–6 Early-to-middle problem drinker At/above 5 — problem drinking indicated
7–24 Alcoholic range (per Selzer 1975) 7+ — dependence assessment indicated

Usage & licensing

Free — Public-domain (Selzer 1975); widely reproduced for clinical/research use.
