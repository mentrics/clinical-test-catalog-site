IIEF — plain-language guide

What it measures: Male sexual function (Sexuality)

Who it's for: Adult men · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 15 items · **Scores from 5 to 75

What this test is

IIEF (International Index of Erectile Function) is a self-report questionnaire that measures male sexual function. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. IIEF: 15 items in 5 domains — Erectile Function (6 items, 1-5 each), Orgasmic Function (2, 0-5), Sexual Desire (2, 0-5), Intercourse Satisfaction (3, 0-5), Overall Satisfaction (2, 0-5). Domain scores = sums ( Erectile 1-30, others 0-10 or 2-10 per anchors). Total 5-75. Validation (Rosen et al. 1997): alphas .86-.97 across domains. Cutoffs (Rosen et al. 1997; Cappelleri et al. 1999): Erectile Function domain 1-10 severe, 11-16 moderate, 17-25 mild, 26-30 no ED; total <26 for dysfunction (varies by study).

Subscales

  • Erectile Function (6 items) — sum
  • Orgasmic Function (2 items) — sum
  • Sexual Desire (2 items) — sum
  • Intercourse Satisfaction (3 items) — sum
  • Overall Satisfaction (2 items) — sum

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
5–25 Erectile dysfunction range Erectile Function domain (1-30): 1-10 severe, 11-16 moderate, 17-25 mild ED; total <26 commonly applied dysfunction threshold (Cappelleri et al. 1999)
26–75 No dysfunction indicated Above conventional ED threshold (Erectile Function 26-30)
