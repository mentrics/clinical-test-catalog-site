ASI-3 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Anxiety sensitivity (Anxiety)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 18 items · **Scores from 0 to 72

What this test is

ASI-3 (Anxiety Sensitivity Index-3) is a self-report questionnaire that measures anxiety sensitivity. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 18 items; also computes 6 sub-facets (3 per higher-order factor): Physical, Cognitive, Social concerns. Validation (Taylor et al. 2007): alpha = .93; ASI-3 superior to ASI/ASI-R in factor structure and reliability. No official diagnostic cutoffs — ASI-3 measures fear-of-fear sensitivity, continuous; higher = greater anxiety sensitivity (risk factor for panic).

Subscales

  • Physical concerns — sum
  • Cognitive concerns — sum
  • Social concerns — sum

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
