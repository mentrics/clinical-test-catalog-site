FSDS-R — plain-language guide

What it measures: Sexual distress (Sexuality)

Who it's for: Adult women · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 13 items · **Scores from 0 to 48

What this test is

FSDS-R (Female Sexual Distress Scale — Revised) is a self-report questionnaire that measures sexual distress. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. FSDS-R: 12 items, single factor (sexual distress). Sum all 12 items. Validation (Derogatis et al. 2002): alpha = .96. Cutoff (Derogatis et al. 2002; Meston et al. 2004): total >=11 indicates clinically significant sexual distress (sensitivity .93, specificity .86 for hypoactive sexual desire disorder).

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–10 Below threshold Sexual distress not clinically significant
11–48 Clinically significant sexual distress At/above 11 — sexual dysfunction evaluation indicated
