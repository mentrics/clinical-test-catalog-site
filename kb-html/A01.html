GAD-7 — plain-language guide

What it measures: General anxiety (Anxiety)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 7 items · **Scores from 0 to 21

What this test is

GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7) is a self-report questionnaire that measures general anxiety. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Not at all (0), Several days (1), More than half the days (2), Nearly every day (3).

Example items (7 total) - Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge - Not being able to stop or control worrying - Worrying too much about different things - Trouble relaxing - Being so restless that it's hard to sit still - … (2 more — see the full item list on the detail page)

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 7 items. Original validation (Spitzer 2006): alpha = .92, test-retest ICC = .83. Cutoff >=10: sensitivity 89%, specificity 82% for GAD. Total score also functions as severity measure across panic, social anxiety, and PTSD.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–4 Minimal No significant anxiety symptoms
5–9 Mild Mild anxiety; monitor
10–14 Moderate Moderate anxiety; consider treatment
15–21 Severe Severe anxiety; treatment strongly indicated
