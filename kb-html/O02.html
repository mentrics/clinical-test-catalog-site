OCI-R — plain-language guide

What it measures: OCD symptoms (OCD)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 18 items · **Scores from 0 to 72

What this test is

OCI-R (Obsessive-Compulsive Inventory-Revised) is a self-report questionnaire that measures ocd symptoms. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. 18 items in 6 subscales of 3 items each (Washing, Checking/Reassurance, Ordering, Obsessing, Hoarding, Mental Neutralizing). Sum each subscale (0-12) + total (0-72). Validation (Foa et al. 2002): alphas .75-.91 across subscales; OCI-R discriminates OCD patients from controls and other anxiety disorders.

Subscales

  • Washing — sum
  • Checking/Reassurance — sum
  • Ordering — sum
  • Obsessing — sum
  • Hoarding — sum
  • Mental Neutralizing — sum

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–20 Below clinical threshold (informal) Below common OCI-R cutoff in OCD screening
21–72 Elevated obsessive-compulsive symptoms (informal) At/above 21 — commonly applied research cutoff for probable OCD
