SCL-90-R — plain-language guide

What it measures: Global symptom burden (Clinical)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 90 items · **Scores from 0 to 4

What this test is

SCL-90-R (Symptom Checklist-90-Revised) is a self-report questionnaire that measures global symptom burden. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: mean. SCL-90-R: 90 items, 9 primary dimensions + 3 global indices. Dimensions (each scored as mean, 0-4): Somatization, Obsessive-Compulsive, Interpersonal Sensitivity, Depression, Anxiety, Hostility, Phobic Anxiety, Paranoid Ideation, Psychoticism. Global indices: GSI (overall mean, most-used), PSI (positive-symptom total), PSDI (positive-symptom distress index). Scores interpreted as T-scores against gender-specific normative samples (T >=63 = clinically elevated, conventional). Validation (Derogatis 1977; Derogatis & Savitz 1994): alphas .77-.90 across dimensions.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–4 T-score interpretation Dimensions interpreted as T-scores: T 63-65 = clinically elevated (1.5 SD above norm mean per Derogatis convention); use official gender-specific norm tables for conversion.
