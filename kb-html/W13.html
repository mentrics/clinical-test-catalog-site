PERMA-Profiler — plain-language guide

What it measures: Psychological flourishing (Well-being)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 23 items · **Scores from 0 to 10

What this test is

PERMA-Profiler (PERMA-Profiler) is a self-report questionnaire that measures psychological flourishing. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: mean. PERMA-Profiler: 15 items in 5 subscales of 3 items each (Positive emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment) + 1 general well-being item + 1 health item (not scored in PERMA total). Subscale scores = means 0-10; PERMA total = mean of the 5 subscales. Validation (Butler & Kern 2016): alphas .90-.94 across subscales; confirmatory factor structure supported. No clinical cutoffs — normative interpretation.

Subscales

  • Positive emotion — mean
  • Engagement — mean
  • Relationships — mean
  • Meaning — mean
  • Accomplishment — mean

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for research/clinical use with attribution (Butler & Kern 2016); permaprofiler.com.
