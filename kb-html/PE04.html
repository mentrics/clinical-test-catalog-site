NEO-FFI-3 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Big Five (Personality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 60 items · **Scores from 0 to 240

What this test is

NEO-FFI-3 (NEO Five-Factor Inventory-3) is a self-report questionnaire that measures big five. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. NEO-FFI-3: 60 items, 5 domains x 12 items. Domain scores = sums after reverse-keying (about half the items per domain). N (0-48), E (0-48), O (0-48), A (0-48), C (0-48), total 0-240 reported as T-scores against gender-specific norms. Validation (Costa & McCrae 1992 for NEO-PI-R basis; NEO-FFI-3 update 2004): domain alphas .84-.90. T-scores from official PAR norms are primary output.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
