SITBI — plain-language guide

What it measures: Self-injury (Suicide/Self-harm)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Interview

Length: Variable items · **Scores from 0 to 7

What this test is

SITBI (Self-Injurious Thoughts and Behaviors Interview) is a interview questionnaire that measures self-injury. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. SITBI is an INTERVIEW yielding presence/absence + frequency/descriptive detail per SITB category — not a summed severity score. Outputs: binary presence per category, onset/age-of-onset, frequency, method, severity (clinician-rated for attempts), medical treatment required. Validation (Nock et al. 2007): inter-rater kappas .83-.99 across categories; test-retest kappa .70-.99.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–7 Per-category presence No severity sum — each of the 7 SITB categories reported independently with frequency/descriptive detail. Clinical interpretation per category, not total.
