PHQ-15 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Somatic symptom burden (Clinical)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 15 items · **Scores from 0 to 30

What this test is

PHQ-15 (Patient Health Questionnaire-15) is a self-report questionnaire that measures somatic symptom burden. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 15 items (15 physical/somatic symptoms). Validation (Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams 2002): discriminates somatization severity in primary care. Cutoffs (Kroenke et al. 2002): 0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15+ severe somatic symptoms; >=10 identifies probable somatoform disorder (sensitivity .88, specificity .73).

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–4 Minimal somatic symptoms No significant somatization
5–9 Mild somatic symptoms Mild; monitor
10–14 Moderate somatic symptoms At/above 10 — probable somatoform disorder; assess
15–30 Severe somatic symptoms Severe; active clinical management indicated

Usage & licensing
