Stroop Color-Word Test — plain-language guide

What it measures: Stroop inhibition (Attention/Executive)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Performance

Length: 3 items · **Scores from 0 to 3

What this test is

Stroop Color-Word Test (Stroop Color-Word Test) is a performance questionnaire that measures stroop inhibition. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. Stroop: 3 conditions (Word naming, Color naming, Color-Word interference). Scores = completion time + errors per condition. The derived measure of interest is the INTERFERENCE score (Color-Word time minus expected non-interference time — e.g., Golden's predicted formula or the simple Color-Word minus Color difference), indexing inhibitory control. Norms: age- and education-corrected (Golden 1978; Strauss et al. 2006 compendium). Higher interference time = worse inhibitory control.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–3 Age/education norm-referenced Interference score interpreted against age/education-adjusted norms (Golden 1978; Strauss et al. 2006) — raw times alone are not diagnostic.

Usage & licensing

Free — Classic Stroop paradigm in the public domain; many published variants (Golden 1978; Trenerry et al. 1989 VST) — specific card sets may carry copyright.
