SADQ — plain-language guide

What it measures: Alcohol dependence (Substance)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 20 items · **Scores from 0 to 45

What this test is

SADQ (Severity of Alcohol Dependence Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures alcohol dependence. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. SADQ (20 items): 5 subscales of 4 items each — Physical withdrawal (4), Affective withdrawal (4), Withdrawal relief drinking (4), Replacement drinking pattern (4), Drink-loci (4). Sum all 20. Validation (Stockwell et al. 1994): alpha = .85+. Cutoffs (Stockwell et al. 1994): 3-15 mild dependence, 16-30 moderate, 31-45 severe alcohol dependence.

Subscales
