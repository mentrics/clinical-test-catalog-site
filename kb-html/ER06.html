MAAS — plain-language guide

What it measures: Mindfulness (Emotion Regulation)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 15 items · **Scores from 1 to 6

What this test is

MAAS (Mindful Attention Awareness Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures mindfulness. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: mean. MAAS: 15 items, single factor (dispositional mindfulness). Score = mean after reverse-keying ALL items (items are worded as automatic/unmindful behaviors — high agreement = LOW mindfulness, so all items reverse). Validation (Brown & Ryan 2003): alpha = .82-.89 across samples. Higher = greater dispositional mindfulness. No clinical cutoffs.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Freely available for research use with attribution (Brown & Ryan 2003).

Source
