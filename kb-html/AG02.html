Cook-Medley Hostility Scale — plain-language guide

What it measures: Hostility (Anger)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 50 items · **Scores from 0 to 50

What this test is

Cook-Medley Hostility Scale (Cook-Medley Hostility Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures hostility. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

How it's scored

Method: sum. Ho Scale: 50 items (27 scored + 23 fillers in the original MMPI-derived version). Sum of scored items keyed 'true' (higher = greater hostility). Validation (Cook & Medley 1954; Barefoot et al. 1989 psychometric update): test-retest r = .84 (6 months); Ho predicts cardiovascular outcomes (Barefoot et al. 1983). 6-item Ho subscales proposed (cynicism, hostile affect, aggressive responding, social avoidance, etc.) with modest reliability. No clinical cutoffs.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
