PBQ-SF — plain-language guide

What it measures: Personality beliefs (Personality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 65 items · **Scores from 0 to 260

What this test is

PBQ-SF (Personality Belief Questionnaire — Short Form) is a self-report questionnaire that measures personality beliefs. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. PBQ-SF has 65 items keyed to 10 DSM PD belief scales (avoidant, dependent, passive-aggressive, obsessive-compulsive, antisocial, narcissistic, histrionic, schizoid, paranoid, borderline) — 6-7 items per scale. Scale scores = sums. Important audit finding (consolidate.md): Rilev presents 7 broader 'belief domains' (social rejection/inadequacy, reliance, control/standards, approval/attention, self-importance/entitlement, mistrust, abandonment/identity) that are NOT the original 10 PBQ-SF scales — that's a Rilev-specific factor grouping, not published scoring. Beck Institute's official scoring uses the 10 scales.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
