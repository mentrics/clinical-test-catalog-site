SDI-2 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Sexual desire (Sexuality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 14 items · **Scores from 0 to 51

What this test is

SDI-2 (Sexual Desire Inventory-2) is a self-report questionnaire that measures sexual desire. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. SDI-2: 14 items, 2 subscales — Dyadic Desire (items involving partner-directed desire, 10 items, 0-8 scale range per item) and Solitary Desire (4 items). Subscale scores = sums; total (0-51 or higher depending on variant — VERIFY against the specific scoring sheet). Validation (Spector, Carey & Speroff 1996): alphas Dyadic .91, Solitary .86. Audit note (consolidate.md): Rilev uses SDI-2 — the AGENTS.md version-ambiguity list flags this. Multiple SDI-2 item sets/scoring sheets exist in the literature.

Subscales

  • Dyadic Desire — sum
  • Solitary Desire — sum

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.
