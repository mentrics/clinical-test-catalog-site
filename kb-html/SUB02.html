AUDIT-C — plain-language guide

What it measures: Alcohol screening (Substance)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 3 items · **Scores from 0 to 12

What this test is

AUDIT-C (Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test — Consumption) is a self-report questionnaire that measures alcohol screening. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum 3 consumption items. Validation (Bush et al. 1998): sensitivity .96, specificity .79 for hazardous drinking at >=4 (men) / >=3 (women) in primary care; AUROC comparable to full AUDIT for screening (0.89-0.91). Sex-specific cutoffs.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–3 Below threshold (men) / threshold (women) Below screening threshold for women; men threshold is 4
4–12 Positive screen (men: >=4) Men: 4+ = positive screen; women: 3+ = positive screen

Usage & licensing

Free — WHO-derived (items 1-3 of AUDIT); free for use with attribution.
