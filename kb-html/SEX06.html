CSFQ — plain-language guide

What it measures: Sexual functioning (Sexuality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 36 items · **Scores from 0 to 120

What this test is

CSFQ (Changes in Sexual Functioning Questionnaire (short form)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures sexual functioning. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

How it's scored

Method: sum. CSFQ-14: 14 items, 5 domains — Desire (frequency + interest, 2 items), Arousal — excitement (2), Arousal — pleasure (2), Orgasm — frequency + pleasure (2), Sexual functioning — satisfaction (2). Total = sum (lower = greater sexual dysfunction). Validation (Clayton et al. 1997; Keller et al. 2006 for psychometrics): alphas .74-.87 across domains; sensitive to SSRI-induced sexual dysfunction. Cutoffs (Clayton et al. 1997): total <=41 (men) / <=41 (women) indicates sexual dysfunction (sex-specific norms differ by version).

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–41 Sexual dysfunction indicated Below sex-specific cutoff — sexual dysfunction evaluation indicated (verify exact cutoff against the CSFQ-14 scoring sheet)
