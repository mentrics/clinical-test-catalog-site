HEXACO-60 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Six-factor traits (Personality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 60 items · **Scores from 1 to 5

What this test is

HEXACO-60 (HEXACO-60 Personality Inventory) is a self-report questionnaire that measures six-factor traits. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: mean. 6 domain scores (Honesty-Humility, Emotionality, eXtraversion, Agreeableness, Conscientiousness, Openness), each = mean of 10 items after reverse-keying (~half the items per scale). The 60-item form scores 6 domains only (the 100-item version adds 4 facets/scale; HEXACO-60 has no facet scores). Validation (Ashton & Lee 2009): domain alphas .72-.80. No clinical cutoffs — normative interpretation. Honest-Humility is the HEXACO model's signature sixth factor absent from Big Five.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for research and clinical use with attribution (Ashton & Lee, hexaco.org).

Source
