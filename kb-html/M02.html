BDI-II — plain-language guide

What it measures: Depression symptoms (Mood)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 21 items · **Scores from 0 to 63

What this test is

BDI-II (Beck Depression Inventory-II) is a self-report questionnaire that measures depression symptoms. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 21 items. BDI-II norms: alpha = .92 in psychiatric outpatients, .93 in college students (Beck, Steer & Brown 1996 manual). Cutoffs from the manual, widely applied. Items 2, 9, 16 also have option-3 responses about suicide/self-harm that warrant clinical attention beyond the total.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–13 Minimal Minimal depression
14–19 Mild Mild depression
20–28 Moderate Moderate depression
29–63 Severe Severe depression
