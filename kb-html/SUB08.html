HSI — plain-language guide

What it measures: Nicotine dependence (Substance)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 2 items · **Scores from 0 to 6

What this test is

HSI (Heaviness of Smoking Index) is a self-report questionnaire that measures nicotine dependence. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. HSI: 2 items (the 2 most discriminating FTND items — time to first cigarette after waking, cigarettes per day). Sum both. Validation (Heatherton et al. 1991; Borrelli et al. 2010 psychometric confirmation): HSI correlates strongly with FTND total and predicts cessation outcomes. Cutoffs: 0-2 low dependence, 3-4 moderate, 5-6 high (by convention from FTND bands scaled down).

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–2 Low dependence Low nicotine-dependence indicators
3–4 Moderate dependence Moderate dependence
5–6 High dependence High dependence; cessation support indicated

Usage & licensing

Free — Freely available for research use with attribution (Heatherton, Kozlowski, Frecker & Fagerström 1991 — HSI is the 2-item subset of the FTND).
