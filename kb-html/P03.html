PQ-B — plain-language guide

What it measures: Psychosis-risk screen (Psychosis/Bipolar)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 21 items · **Scores from 0 to 1

What this test is

PQ-B (Prodromal Questionnaire — Brief) is a self-report questionnaire that measures psychosis-risk screen. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. PQ-B: 21 yes/no items. POSITIVE SCREEN requires >=8 items endorsed yes (primary threshold from Loewy et al. 2011 validation: 8+ items, sensitivity .95, specificity .50 — deliberately high sensitivity for follow-up interview); distress ratings refine triage but do not change the screen threshold. PQ-B is a screening instrument — positive screen → SIPS/SIPS-5 clinical interview.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–7 Screen negative Fewer than 8 items endorsed — prodromal screening criteria not met
8–21 Screen positive 8+ items endorsed — clinical interview (SIPS/CAARMS) indicated
