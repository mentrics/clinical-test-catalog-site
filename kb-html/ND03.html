RAADS-R — plain-language guide

What it measures: Autistic traits (Neurodevelopment)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 80 items · **Scores from 0 to 5

What this test is

RAADS-R (Ritvo Autism Asperger Diagnostic Scale-Revised) is a self-report questionnaire that measures autistic traits. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. 80 items total: 64 self-report symptom items + 16 demographic/cognitive items. The 64 symptom items produce a total score plus 5 subscales (social relatedness, circumscribed interests, sensory-movement, communication, self-regulation). Total score bands distinguish autism-probable / autism-possible / non-autistic per Ritvo et al. (2011) validation: total cutoff approximately 65+ = probable autism in high-functioning adults (scale-specific norm tables required).

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–0 Non-autistic range Below validation threshold
1–5 Autism-possible / probable See RAADS-R norm tables — banding requires the official score sheet; clinical interview required for diagnosis
