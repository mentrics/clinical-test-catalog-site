WHOQOL-BREF — plain-language guide

What it measures: Quality of life (Quality of Life)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 26 items · **Scores from 4 to 20

What this test is

WHOQOL-BREF (WHO Quality of Life-BREF) is a self-report questionnaire that measures quality of life. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: mean. WHOQOL-BREF: 26 items → 4 domains (Physical health 7 items, Psychological 6, Social relationships 3, Environment 8) + 2 single-item overall QoL items. Domain scores = mean of domain items x 4 (transformed to 4-20 range, higher = better QoL), OR transformed to 0-100. About half the items reverse-keyed per official key. Validation (WHOQOL Group 1998): alphas .66-.84 across domains. No clinical cutoffs — normative interpretation.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for clinical/research use with attribution (WHOQOL Group 1998).

Source
