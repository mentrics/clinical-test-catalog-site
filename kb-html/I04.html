STAXI-2 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Anger (Interpersonal)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 57 items · **Scores from 8 to 32

What this test is

STAXI-2 (State-Trait Anger Expression Inventory-2 (short name in catalog)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures anger. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. STAXI-2 (57 items): State Anger (10 items), Trait Anger (10), Anger Expression-Out (8), Anger Expression-In (8), Anger Control-Out (6), Anger Control-In (6), Anger Expression Index (composite). Scale scores = sums interpreted as T-scores against gender-specific norms. Validation (Spielberger 1999): alphas .73-.93 across scales. T >=70 = clinically elevated.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
8–32 T-score interpretation Scales interpreted as T-scores (M = 50, SD = 10); T >=70 = clinically elevated. Official STAXI-2 norm tables required for conversion.
