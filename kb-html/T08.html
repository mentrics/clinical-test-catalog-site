CTQ-SF — plain-language guide

What it measures: Child trauma (Trauma)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 28 items · **Scores from 25 to 125

What this test is

CTQ-SF (Childhood Trauma Questionnaire — Short Form) is a self-report questionnaire that measures child trauma. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. 5 subscales of 5 items each (Emotional Abuse, Physical Abuse, Sexual Abuse, Emotional Neglect, Physical Neglect), each 5-25; total 25-125. 3 items serve as a Minimization/Denial validity scale. About half the items are reverse-keyed — official key required. Validation (Bernstein et al. 2003): alphas .66-.94 (sexual abuse lowest); cutoffs from Bernstein 2003 norm tables per scale (none/minor/moderate/severe per subscale).

Subscales

  • Emotional abuse — 5 items
  • Physical abuse — 5 items
  • Sexual abuse — 5 items
  • Emotional neglect — 5 items
  • Physical neglect — 5 items

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
25–125 Per-subscale norm bands CTQ reports per-subscale severity (none/minor/moderate/severe) using Bernstein et al. (2003) norm tables — no single accepted total cutoff. See manual for per-scale band boundaries.
