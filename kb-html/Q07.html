WHODAS 2.0 12-item — plain-language guide

What it measures: Clinical impairment (Functioning)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 12 items · **Scores from 0 to 48

What this test is

WHODAS 2.0 12-item (WHODAS 2.0 12-item version) is a self-report questionnaire that measures clinical impairment. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. WHODAS 2.0 12-item version: 12 items sampled from the 6 domains of the 36-item version (2 per domain). Score = sum (0-48) or IRT-transformed 0-100 (WHO scoring sheet). Validation (Ustün et al. 2010): 12-item correlates .97 with 36-item total; suitable when brevity is essential. Higher = greater disability.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for clinical/research use (WHO, Ustün et al. 2010).
