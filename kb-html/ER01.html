ERQ — plain-language guide

What it measures: Emotion regulation (Emotion Regulation)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 10 items · **Scores from 1 to 7

What this test is

ERQ (Emotion Regulation Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures emotion regulation. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Strongly disagree (1), Disagree (2), Somewhat disagree (3), Neither agree nor disagree (4), Somewhat agree (5), Agree (6), Strongly agree (7).

Example items (10 total) - When I want to feel more positive emotion (such as joy or amusement), I change what I'm thinking about. - I keep my emotions to myself. - When I want to feel less negative emotion (such as sadness or anger), I change what I'm thinking about. - When I am feeling positive emotions, I am careful not to express them. - When I'm faced with a stressful situation, I make myself think about it in a way that helps me stay calm. - … (5 more — see the full item list on the detail page)

How it's scored

Method: mean. Two independent subscale means (do NOT sum the 10 items into a total): Cognitive Reappraisal (6 items) and Expressive Suppression (4 items). Original validation (Gross & John 2003): alphas reappraisal = .88, suppression = .85. Publication norm: report each subscale mean (1-7); no total score, no clinical cutoffs — interpret relative to sample norms (e.g., US college mean reappraisal ≈ 4.6, suppression ≈ 3.0). ERQ: no clinical cutoffs — subscale means interpreted vs sample norms (Gross & John 2003: US college reappraisal ~4.6, suppression ~3.0). Bands below are runner-convenience heuristics around those norms.

Subscales

  • Cognitive Reappraisal — items 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10
  • Expressive Suppression — items 2, 4, 6, 8

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
1–3 Low (informal) Below typical college-sample norms
4–6 Typical range (informal) Within typical sample range
7–7 High (informal) Above typical sample range
