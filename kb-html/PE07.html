SCID-5-PD — plain-language guide

What it measures: Personality pathology (Personality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Clinician interview

Length: Variable items · **Scores from 0 to 10

What this test is

SCID-5-PD (Structured Clinical Interview for DSM-5 Personality Disorders) is a clinician interview questionnaire that measures personality pathology. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. SCID-5-PD is a clinician-administered INTERVIEW. Each PD (borderline, antisocial, narcissistic, avoidant, dependent, obsessive-compulsive, schizotypal, schizoid, histrionic, paranoid) is diagnosed when a defined number of its criteria are rated 2 (e.g., BPD: 5+ of 9). Outputs are categorical PD diagnoses (meet/do-not-meet) + optional dimensional counts. Also computes a total criterion count for severity.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–10 Per-PD criterion thresholds Each PD has its own DSM-5 criterion threshold (e.g., 5 of 9 for BPD, 4 of 7 for avoidant, 3 of 7 for schizoid, etc. per DSM-5 criteria sets). See SCID-5-PD manual for each PD's exact count.
