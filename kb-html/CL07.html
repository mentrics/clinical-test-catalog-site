MSI-BPD — plain-language guide

What it measures: Borderline symptoms (Clinical)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 1

What this test is

MSI-BPD (McLean Screening Instrument for Borderline Personality Disorder) is a self-report questionnaire that measures borderline symptoms. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. MSI-BPD: POSITIVE SCREEN requires >=7 of 10 BPD-criterion items endorsed yes AND item 11 (impairment) endorsed yes. Validation (Zanarini et al. 2003): sensitivity .81, specificity .84 vs. SCID-BPD at the 7+ threshold. Screening instrument — positive screen → full clinical interview for BPD.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–0 Screen negative Fewer than 7 criteria endorsed or no impairment — BPD screening criteria not met
1–1 Screen positive 7+ criteria + impairment — full BPD assessment indicated
