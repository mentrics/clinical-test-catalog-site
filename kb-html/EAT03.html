BES — plain-language guide

What it measures: Binge eating (Eating)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 16 items · **Scores from 0 to 48

What this test is

BES (Binge Eating Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures binge eating. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 16 items. Validation (Gormally et al. 1982): discriminates binge eaters from non-binge eaters; internal consistency reported .85+ in later studies. Cutoffs (Gormally et al. 1982): <=17 non-bingeing, 18-26 moderate bingeing, >=27 severe bingeing.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–17 No/non-bingeing Below moderate-binge threshold
18–26 Moderate bingeing Moderate binge-eating pattern indicated
27–48 Severe bingeing Severe binge-eating pattern; clinical assessment indicated

Usage & licensing

Free — Freely available for research use with attribution (Gormally, Black, Daston & Rardin 1982).
