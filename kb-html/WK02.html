WPAI — plain-language guide

What it measures: Work productivity (Work)

Who it's for: Workers · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 6 items · **Scores from 0 to 100

What this test is

WPAI (Work Productivity and Activity Impairment Questionnaire (General Health version)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures work productivity. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. WPAI-GH produces PERCENT impairment scores, not sums: Absenteeism % = hours missed / (hours missed + hours worked) x 100; Presenteeism % = work-affected rating / 10 x 100; Work productivity loss % = absenteeism + (presenteeism x (1 - absenteeism)); Activity impairment % = activity-affected rating / 10 x 100. Validation (Reilly et al. 1993): discriminates by health status; widely used in health-economics outcomes research. Higher % = greater impairment.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for research use with attribution (Reilly et al. 1993; WPAI available from the developers).
