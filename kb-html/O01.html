Y-BOCS-II — plain-language guide

What it measures: OCD severity (OCD)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Clinician

Length: 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 40

What this test is

Y-BOCS-II (Yale-Brown Obsessive Compulsive Scale — Second Edition) is a clinician questionnaire that measures ocd severity. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 10 items (obsessions 0-20 + compulsions 0-20). Y-BOCS-II adds a 'yes/no' compulsive-behavior screen and insight item (not summed in the 0-40 total). Validation (Storch et al. 2010 for Y-BOCS-II): alpha = .84-.93, excellent inter-rater reliability. Severity bands from Storch et al. (2010) update — slightly stricter than original Y-BOCS bands.

Subscales

  • Obsessions (items 1-5) — sum
  • Compulsions (items 6-10) — sum

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–19 Subclinical to moderate OCD Below severe range (Y-BOCS-II updated bands)
20–29 Moderate to severe OCD At/above Y-BOCS-II 20+ for more severe symptoms
30–40 Severe OCD Severe OCD; intensive treatment indicated
