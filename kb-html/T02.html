IES-R — plain-language guide

What it measures: PTSD (Trauma)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 22 items · **Scores from 0 to 88

What this test is

IES-R (Impact of Event Scale-Revised) is a self-report questionnaire that measures ptsd. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 22 items, plus 3 subscales: Intrusion (8 items), Avoidance (8 items), Hyperarousal (6 items). Validation (Weiss & Marmar 1997; Creamer et al. 2003): alphas .79-.94 across subscales. Cutoffs (Creamer et al. 2003): total 33+ PTSD-probable threshold (sensitivity .89, specificity .94 in trauma-exposed samples); subscale cutoffs 8+ intrusion, 8+ avoidance, 19+ hyperarousal. Coverage fixed 2026-09-09: band extended to 88 (full range) — previous max was 87.

Subscales
