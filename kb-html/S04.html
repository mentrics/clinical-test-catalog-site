PROMIS Sleep Disturbance 8a — plain-language guide

What it measures: Sleep disturbance (Sleep)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 8 items · **Scores from 0 to 100

What this test is

PROMIS Sleep Disturbance 8a (PROMIS Sleep Disturbance 8a (v2.0)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures sleep disturbance. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. PROMIS uses IRT-based T-score scoring: item responses are mapped to a theta score via the PROMIS scoring service (Assessment Center / healthmeasures lookup tables), then standardized to a T-metric (mean 50, SD 10, US general population). Higher T = more sleep disturbance. 8a is the 8-item short form (static full bank: 27 items). Validation (Yu et al. 2011/2012 PROMIS sleep papers): alpha ~.93, responsiveness established. Cutoffs: T 50 = population mean; T >=60 (1 SD above mean) commonly flagged as clinically elevated.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–59 Within normal range (T<60) Sleep disturbance at/below 1 SD above the population mean
60–69 Elevated (T 60-69) 1-2 SD above mean — clinically notable disturbance
70–100 Very elevated (T>=70) 2+ SD above mean — severe disturbance
