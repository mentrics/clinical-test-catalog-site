LSNS-6 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Social network (Social)

Who it's for: Older adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 6 items · **Scores from 0 to 30

What this test is

LSNS-6 (Lubben Social Network Scale-6) is a self-report questionnaire that measures social network. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 6 items (2 subscales: Family network items 1-3, Friendships items 4-6). Validation (Lubben et al. 2006): alpha = .82-.86; cutoff <=12 indicates social isolation risk (sensitivity/specificity profile in Lubben 2006 Table 3). Item 6 (dependence on others for emergency care) keyed separately in some protocols.

Subscales

  • Family network — items 1, 2, 3
  • Friendship network — items 4, 5, 6

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–12 Social isolation risk At/below 12 — elevated social isolation risk (original cutoff)
13–30 Adequate social network Above isolation-risk threshold

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for research/clinical use with attribution (Lubben et al. 2006); widely used in geriatric/public-health assessment.

Source

Lubben, J., Blozik, E., Gillmann, G., et al. (2006). Performance of an abbreviated version of the Lubben Social Network Scale among three European community-dwelling older adult populations. Gerontologist, 46(4), 503-513. · DOI
