PHQ-8 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Depression (Mood)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 8 items · **Scores from 0 to 24

What this test is

PHQ-8 (PHQ-8) is a self-report questionnaire that measures depression. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

What the questions look like

Each item is answered on one scale: Not at all (0), Several days (1), More than half the days (2), Nearly every day (3).

Example items (8 total) - Little interest or pleasure in doing things - Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless - Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much - Feeling tired or having little energy - Poor appetite or overeating - … (3 more — see the full item list on the detail page)

How it's scored

Method: sum. PHQ-9 minus item 9 (suicidal ideation) — used when item 9 is handled separately (e.g., population surveys like BRFSS, telephone administration). Sum all 8 items; cutoffs analogized from PHQ-9 but bands shifted (0-24 range). Validation: Kroenke et al. (2001) PHQ-9 psychometrics apply to the shared items; PHQ-8-specific validation in Martin et al. (2006), BRFSS 2006 onward.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–4 Minimal Minimal depression
5–9 Mild Mild depression
10–14 Moderate Moderate depression
15–19 Moderately severe Moderately severe depression
20–24 Severe Severe depression
