PSWQ — plain-language guide

What it measures: Worry (Anxiety)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 16 items · **Scores from 16 to 80

What this test is

PSWQ (Penn State Worry Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures worry. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 16 items (5 items reverse-keyed: items 2, 4, 9, 10, 14 — official key required). Validation (Meyer et al. 1990): alpha = .86-.94; test-retest r = .93 (8-10 weeks). Cutoff >=45 commonly used to indicate probable GAD-level worry (Behar et al. 2003 ROC analysis); >=62/69 for severe worry in some samples.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
16–44 Below clinical worry threshold Worry not prominent
45–61 Probable GAD-level worry At/above 45 — assess for generalized anxiety disorder
62–80 Severe worry Very high worry; clinical evaluation indicated
