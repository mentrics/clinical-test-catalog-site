SIAS — plain-language guide

What it measures: Social anxiety (Anxiety)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 20 items · **Scores from 0 to 80

What this test is

SIAS (Social Interaction Anxiety Scale) is a self-report questionnaire that measures social anxiety. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 19 items (3 items reverse-keyed — official key required; some publications drop 2 items, giving SIAS-17). Validation (Mattick & Clarke 1998; Peters et al. 2012 psychometric update): alpha = .88-.94; SIAS correlates strongly with LSAS and distinguishes social phobia from controls.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–33 Below threshold (informal) Social interaction anxiety not prominent (heuristic; no universal official cutoff)
34–80 Elevated social interaction anxiety (informal) At/above commonly applied 34 threshold in social-phobia research
