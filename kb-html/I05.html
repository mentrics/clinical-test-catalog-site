RAS — plain-language guide

What it measures: Assertiveness (Interpersonal)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 30 items · **Scores from 5 to 35

What this test is

RAS (Relationship Assessment Scale (Interpersonal listing)) is a self-report questionnaire that measures assertiveness. The instrument is established, but there are caveats worth knowing (version, scoring, or derived-score issues).

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. RAS: 7 items (item 2 reverse-keyed), sum 5-35. Validation (Hendrick 1988): alpha = .86. Higher = more relationship satisfaction; no universal clinical cutoff (some research uses <=20 for distressed relationships). Catalog lists RAS twice (REL05 Relationships and I05 Interpersonal) — duplicate catalog entry.

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Freely available for research use with attribution (Hendrick 1988).
