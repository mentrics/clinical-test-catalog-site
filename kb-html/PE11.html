NPI-16 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Narcissistic traits (Personality)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 16 items · **Scores from 0 to 16

What this test is

NPI-16 (Narcissistic Personality Inventory-16) is a self-report questionnaire that measures narcissistic traits. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. NPI-16 (Ames, Rose & Anderson 2006): 16 forced-choice pairs; narcissistic choice = 1. Sum all 16. Validation: NPI-16 correlates .96 with full NPI-40; alphas ~.80+. Interpretation is continuous (higher = more narcissistic-trait endorsement); no clinical cutoff — the NPI is a trait measure, NOT a narcissistic-personality-disorder diagnostic (Ames et al. 2006 explicitly note this).

What the scores mean

There is no official cutoff — this instrument produces a continuous score that is interpreted relative to comparison groups rather than a pass/fail threshold.

Usage & licensing

Free — Freely available for research use (Raskin & Terry 1988; NPI-16 Ames et al. 2006).
