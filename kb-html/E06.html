Trail Making Test — plain-language guide

What it measures: Executive function (Attention/Executive)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Performance

Length: 2 items · **Scores from 0 to 300

What this test is

Trail Making Test (Trail Making Test (A and B)) is a performance questionnaire that measures executive function. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. TMT-A score = completion time in seconds (max 180s for A); TMT-B = completion time (max 300s for B); errors add time per convention. The derived measure of interest is the B-A difference (or B/A ratio), indexing cognitive-flexibility/set-shifting demand beyond pure speed. Norms: age-, education-, and sometimes IQ-corrected percentile tables (Tombaugh 2004; Salthouse & Fristoe 1995). Higher time = worse performance.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–300 Age/education norm-referenced TMT-A >50s and TMT-B >120s are rough adult screening flags, but interpretation MUST use age/education-adjusted norm tables (Tombaugh 2004) — raw seconds alone are not diagnostic.

Usage & licensing

Free — Original Army Individual Test Battery (1944) format is in the public domain; widely reproduced in neuropsychology with attribution to Reitan (1958).
