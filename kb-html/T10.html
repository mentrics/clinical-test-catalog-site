TSQ — plain-language guide

What it measures: Trauma symptom severity (Trauma)

Who it's for: Adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 10 items · **Scores from 0 to 1

What this test is

TSQ (Trauma Screening Questionnaire) is a self-report questionnaire that measures trauma symptom severity. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. TSQ (Brewin et al. 2002) — 10 items selected from IES-R (6 re-experiencing) and BSI (4 arousal). POSITIVE SCREEN requires >=6 of 10 items endorsed yes. Validation: sensitivity .86, specificity .93 for DSM-IV PTSD at the 6+ threshold in assault and accident victims. Designed as a brief rescreen after initial positive trauma screen.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–5 Screen negative Fewer than 6 items endorsed — PTSD screening criteria not met
6–10 Screen positive 6+ items endorsed — full PTSD assessment indicated

Usage & licensing

Free — Free for clinical/research use with attribution (Brewin, Rose & Andrews 2002).
