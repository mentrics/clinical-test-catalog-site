BAI — plain-language guide

What it measures: General anxiety (Anxiety)

Who it's for: Adolescents/adults · How it's given: Self-report

Length: 21 items · **Scores from 0 to 63

What this test is

BAI (Beck Anxiety Inventory) is a self-report questionnaire that measures general anxiety. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: sum. Sum all 21 items. Validation (Beck, Epstein, Brown & Steer 1988; Beck & Steer 1990 manual): alpha = .92, test-retest r = .75 (1 week); BAI distinguished anxiety from depression factorially. Cutoffs widely applied from manual and clinical norms.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–7 Minimal anxiety Minimal anxiety
8–15 Mild anxiety Mild anxiety
16–25 Moderate anxiety Moderate anxiety
26–63 Severe anxiety Severe anxiety
