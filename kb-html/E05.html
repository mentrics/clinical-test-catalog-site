BRIEF-2 — plain-language guide

What it measures: Executive function (Attention/Executive)

Who it's for: Children/adolescents · How it's given: Informant

Length: 63 items · **Scores from 0 to 9

What this test is

BRIEF-2 (Behavior Rating Inventory of Executive Function-2) is a informant questionnaire that measures executive function. This instrument is well validated in the research literature.

Item text

The exact question wording is not reproduced here (licensing/copyright). The scoring logic below is still fully documented.

How it's scored

Method: algorithm. BRIEF-2 (parent/teacher 86 items; self-report 77 items): 9 clinical scales (Inhibit, Shift, Emotional Control, Self-Monitor, Initiate, Working Memory, Plan/Organize, Task Monitor, Organization of Materials) → 2 index scores (BRI, ERI) + Global Executive Composite. Scale scores = raw sums converted to T-scores against age/gender/rater norms (M = 50, SD = 10); T >=65 = clinically elevated. Validation (Gioia et al. 2000 BRIEF original; Guy et al. 2004 self-report): internal consistency .80-.98 across scales.

What the scores mean

Score range Band What it means
0–9 T-score interpretation Scales + indices interpreted as T-scores; T >=65 = clinically elevated executive dysfunction. Official BRIEF-2 norm tables (age/gender/rater strata) required for conversion.
